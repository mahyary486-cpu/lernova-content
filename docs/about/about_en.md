# About Learnova

**Learnova: a teacher that is always with you.**

Learnova teaches mathematics, physics and chemistry (and biology in countries that have it) for grades 9 to 12 of high school. The lessons of each country are written in the language of that country and follow that country's national curriculum.

Our main goal is educational equity. We want good education to reach everyone, including students in remote areas with weak or no internet access.

## What you get

- Lessons in your own language, matched to your country's official curriculum.
- Multiple-choice quizzes at three levels (easy, medium and hard), each with a detailed explanation of the answer.
- Questions to AI assistants, online and offline.
- Online rooms with a teacher. The teacher chooses and manages the room.

## Price

The lessons, the quizzes and the My AI assistant cost the student about the same as a one-dollar purchase. Only the online room is a Pro feature, because the services behind it are expensive to run.

## Why Learnova

- Low price and worldwide use.
- Content in the language of each country.
- Made for classrooms and areas with a weak internet connection.
- Tested on high-school mathematics, with top results.
