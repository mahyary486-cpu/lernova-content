I'll call you as soon as I ___ there.|get|will get|got|getting|0|时间状语从句，主将从现，从句用一般现在时。
___ you hurry, you'll be late.|If|Unless|Although|Because|1|意思是"除非你快点，否则你会迟到"，用 unless。
He was ___ tired that he fell asleep at once.|so|such|very|too|0|tired 是形容词，用 so ... that。
It was ___ a nice day that we went out.|so|such|very|quite|1|a nice day 含有名词，用 such a ... that。
___ it was raining, they went out.|Because|But|Although|So|2|语义转折（下雨却出去了），用 although。
He studied hard ___ he could pass the exam.|so that|such that|because|but|0|表示目的（以便他能通过考试），用 so that。
He didn't go to bed ___ his mother came back.|when|until|while|after|1|not ... until 表示"直到……才……"。
Which sentence is correct?|Although he is poor, but he is happy.|Although he is poor, he is happy.|He is poor, although but he is happy.|Because he is poor, so he is unhappy.|1|although 和 but、because 和 so 都不能连用。
