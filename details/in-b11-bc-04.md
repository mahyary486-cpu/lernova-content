# Viruses, viroids, prions and lichens

Viruses (discovered by Ivanowsky) have a nucleic acid and a protein coat; bacteriophages infect bacteria. Viroids (Diener) are free RNA and prions are infectious proteins (mad cow disease). Lichens are symbiotic associations of algae and fungi and mycorrhiza is a fungus with plant roots.

HIV and TMV have RNA as genetic material.
