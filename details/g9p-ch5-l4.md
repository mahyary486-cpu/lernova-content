# The Electromagnetic Spectrum

Light is only a small part of a family of waves that also includes radio, X-rays and more. All of them travel at the same speed in a vacuum.

## Key ideas
- Electromagnetic waves are **transverse** and travel through a **vacuum**.
- Speed of light: **c = 3 x 10^8 m/s**.
- Order from low to high frequency: **radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma**.
- Higher frequency means **shorter wavelength** and **more energy**.
- Uses: radio (broadcasting), microwaves (cooking, phones), infrared (heat, remote controls), UV (sterilizing), X-rays (medical images).
- Within visible light red has the longest wavelength and violet the shortest.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Wavelength of a radio wave.** A radio station broadcasts at 100 MHz (1 x 10^8 Hz).
1. lambda = c / f = 3 x 10^8 / 1 x 10^8.
2. lambda = 3 m.

**Example 2: Light from the Sun.** Sunlight takes about 500 s to reach Earth.
1. d = c t = 3 x 10^8 x 500.
2. d = 1.5 x 10^11 m.

**Example 3: Comparing waves.** Which has more energy: infrared or ultraviolet?
1. Ultraviolet has a higher frequency than infrared.
2. So ultraviolet waves carry more energy.

## Common mistakes
- Thinking radio waves are sound waves.
- Thinking light needs a medium.
- Thinking red light has a higher frequency than blue light.
