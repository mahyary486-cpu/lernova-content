# The Electromagnetic Spectrum

Visible light is only a small part of a large family of waves. Radio waves, microwaves, X-rays and light all belong to this family, called electromagnetic waves. They have very different uses, yet they share some important properties.

## Key ideas

Electromagnetic waves are transverse waves made of changing electric and magnetic fields. Unlike sound, they do not need a medium and can travel through the vacuum of space; this is how sunlight reaches the Earth. In a vacuum, all electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed, the speed of light, c = 3 x 10^8 m/s. The wave equation v = f x lambda applies to them with v = c.

The family is called the electromagnetic spectrum, and its members are ordered by frequency. From the lowest frequency to the highest, they are radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays. Since the speed is fixed, a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength. Higher frequency also means more energy, which is why ultraviolet light, X-rays and gamma rays are more harmful to living tissue than radio waves.

Each part of the spectrum has typical uses. Radio waves carry radio and television signals. Microwaves are used for cooking and mobile phone communication. Infrared radiation is felt as heat and is used in remote controls and thermal cameras. Visible light is the only part that our eyes can detect; within it, red has the longest wavelength and violet the shortest. Ultraviolet light can sterilize equipment and also causes sunburn. X-rays pass through soft tissue and are used for medical images, and gamma rays are used in medicine and also come from radioactive materials.

## Worked examples

**Example 1: The wavelength of a radio wave.** A radio station broadcasts at 100 MHz, which is 1 x 10^8 Hz. What is the wavelength?
1. Use lambda = c / f.
2. Substitute: lambda = 3 x 10^8 / 1 x 10^8.
3. The wavelength is 3 m.

**Example 2: Light from the Sun.** Sunlight takes about 500 s to reach the Earth. How far away is the Sun?
1. Light travels at c = 3 x 10^8 m/s.
2. Use d = c x t = 3 x 10^8 x 500.
3. The distance is 1.5 x 10^11 m, or 150 million kilometers.

**Example 3: Comparing waves.** Which has more energy, infrared or ultraviolet radiation?
1. In the spectrum, ultraviolet comes after visible light, and infrared comes before it.
2. So ultraviolet has a higher frequency than infrared.
3. Higher frequency means more energy, so ultraviolet waves carry more energy.

## Common mistakes

Some students think that radio waves are sound waves. They are electromagnetic waves that we cannot hear, and a radio changes them into sound. Another mistake is to think that light needs a medium to travel; it does not. A third common error is to believe that red light has a higher frequency than blue light. Blue light has the higher frequency and the shorter wavelength.
