# Types of chemical reactions

Combination: A + B → AB (burning of magnesium). Decomposition: AB → A + B by heat, light or electricity (heating limestone). Displacement: a more reactive element displaces a less reactive one (Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu). Double displacement: exchange of ions (Na₂SO₄ + BaCl₂ → BaSO₄ + 2NaCl).

Exothermic reactions release heat and endothermic reactions absorb heat.
