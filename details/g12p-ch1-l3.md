# Electric Potential and Potential Energy

Potential is to electricity what height is to gravity: charges move from high potential to low potential.

## Key ideas
- **V = k Q / r**; **U = q V**; 1 V = 1 J/C.
- **delta V = W / q**.
- **1 eV = 1.6 x 10^-19 J**.
- Uniform field: **E = V / d**.
- The field is **perpendicular** to equipotential surfaces and points toward **lower** V.
- Potential is a **scalar**: add numbers.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Potential of a charge.** Find the potential 0.3 m from a 2 x 10^-9 C charge.
1. V = k Q / r = 9 x 10^9 x 2 x 10^-9 / 0.3.
2. V = 60 V.

**Example 2: Energy gained.** A proton moves through a potential difference of 500 V.
1. Energy = q delta V = 1.6 x 10^-19 x 500.
2. Energy = 8 x 10^-17 J = 500 eV.

**Example 3: Field between plates.** Two plates 2 cm apart have a potential difference of 400 V.
1. E = V / d = 400 / 0.02.
2. E = 20,000 V/m.

## Common mistakes
- Treating potential as a vector.
- Using centimeters instead of meters.
- Confusing potential with potential energy.
