# Polynomials, degree and zeroes

A polynomial in x has the form aₙxⁿ + … + a₁x + a₀. Its degree is the highest power of x: degree 1 is linear, 2 is quadratic, 3 is cubic.

A real number k is a zero of p(x) if p(k) = 0. For example 2 and 3 are zeroes of x² − 5x + 6. A polynomial of degree n has at most n zeroes.
