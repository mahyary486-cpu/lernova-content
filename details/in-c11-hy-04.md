# Aromatic hydrocarbons

Benzene (C₆H₆) is a planar hexagon with 6 delocalised π electrons and equal C–C bonds of 139 pm; it obeys Hückel's rule (4n + 2). It undergoes electrophilic substitution: nitration (conc. HNO₃ and H₂SO₄), halogenation and Friedel–Crafts reactions (AlCl₃).

Benzene has 12 sigma bonds.
