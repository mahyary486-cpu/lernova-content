# Discovery of the cell and the cell theory

Robert Hooke observed the cells of cork under a microscope in 1665. The cell theory states that all living organisms are made of cells and that the cell is the basic unit of life.

Organisms can be unicellular (amoeba, bacteria) or multicellular (humans, trees). Cells are usually small because a large surface-area-to-volume ratio makes exchange with the surroundings efficient.
