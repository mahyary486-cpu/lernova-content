# Equations of a circle and a line

**Circle.** A circle with center (a; b) and radius r has the equation (x − a)² + (y − b)² = r². For center (2; −3) and r = 4: (x − 2)² + (y + 3)² = 16. For center (0; 0): x² + y² = r².

A point is **on** the circle if its coordinates satisfy the equation, **inside** if the left side is smaller than r², and **outside** if it is larger. The point (3; 4) lies on x² + y² = 25 because 9 + 16 = 25.

**Line.** The equation y = kx + m has slope k. A line through two points has k = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁). Through (0; 1) and (2; 5): k = 2 and y = 2x + 1.

**Intersections.** To find where a line meets a circle, substitute the line into the circle equation and solve the quadratic.
