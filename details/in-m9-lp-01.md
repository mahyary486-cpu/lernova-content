# Polynomials and degree

A polynomial in x is an expression with non-negative whole-number powers of x, such as 3x + 2 or x² − 5x + 6. The numbers multiplying the powers are coefficients; a term without x is the constant term.

The degree is the highest power of x. A non-zero constant has degree 0; the zero polynomial has no defined degree. Expressions like 1/x or √x are not polynomials.
