# Electronic configuration

Orbitals fill in order of increasing energy (Aufbau principle); no two electrons have the same four quantum numbers (Pauli); degenerate orbitals fill singly first (Hund). Half-filled and fully filled subshells are extra stable: Cr is [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ and Cu is [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹.

Example: sodium is 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹.
