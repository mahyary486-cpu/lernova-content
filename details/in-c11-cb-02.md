# VSEPR theory and shapes

Electron pair repulsion decides the shape, with lone pairs repelling more than bond pairs. CH₄ is tetrahedral (109.5°), NH₃ pyramidal (107°), H₂O bent (104.5°), BF₃ trigonal planar (120°), BeCl₂ and CO₂ linear, PCl₅ trigonal bipyramidal, SF₆ octahedral and XeF₄ square planar.

Symmetrical molecules like CO₂ and CCl₄ have zero dipole moment.
