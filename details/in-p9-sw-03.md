# Speed of sound, reflection and echo

Sound travels fastest in solids, slower in liquids and slowest in gases. In air it is about 343 m/s at 20 °C and increases with temperature.

Sound reflects from hard surfaces. Because of persistence of hearing (about 0.1 s) a distinct echo needs a reflector at least about 17 m away. Distance = (speed × time) / 2 for an echo.
