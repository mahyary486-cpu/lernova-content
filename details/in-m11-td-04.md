# Section formula

The point dividing the join of P(x₁, y₁, z₁) and Q(x₂, y₂, z₂) internally in the ratio m : n is ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m + n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m + n), (mz₂ + nz₁)/(m + n)). The midpoint is ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2, (z₁ + z₂)/2) and the centroid of a triangle is the average of the vertices.

Example: the point dividing (1, 2, 3) and (4, 5, 6) in the ratio 1 : 2 is (2, 3, 4).
