# Reflection and Plane and Curved Mirrors

A makeup mirror magnifies your face because it is a concave mirror used close up.

## Key ideas
- **Law of reflection**: angle in = angle out.
- **f = R / 2**.
- **1 / f = 1 / d_o + 1 / d_i**.
- **m = -d_i / d_o**; negative m = inverted.
- Positive d_i: **real** image; negative d_i: **virtual**.
- Convex mirrors have a **negative** focal length and always give virtual, smaller images.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Image position.** An object is 30 cm from a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm.
1. 1 / d_i = 1 / f - 1 / d_o = 1 / 10 - 1 / 30 = 2 / 30.
2. d_i = 15 cm (real).

**Example 2: Magnification.** For the same case:
1. m = -d_i / d_o = -15 / 30.
2. m = -0.5: inverted and half the size.

**Example 3: Close object.** An object is 5 cm from the same mirror.
1. 1 / d_i = 1 / 10 - 1 / 5 = -1 / 10.
2. d_i = -10 cm: a virtual image; m = +2, upright and magnified.

## Common mistakes
- Mixing up real and virtual sign conventions.
- Using the radius instead of f.
- Forgetting the minus sign in m.
