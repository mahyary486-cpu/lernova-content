# Kinetic interpretation of temperature

The average kinetic energy of a molecule is (3/2)kT, depending only on temperature. v_rms = √(3RT/M) ∝ √T; at the same T lighter gases move faster, for example the ratio of rms speeds of H₂ to O₂ is 4 : 1.

The most probable, average and rms speeds are in the order v_p < v_avg < v_rms.
