# Ratios, Proportions and Percent

## Ratios
A ratio compares two quantities. It can be written as 2:3, 2/3, or "2 to 3". Simplify by dividing both parts by their greatest common factor: 12:18 = 2:3.

## Proportions
A proportion states that two ratios are equal. Solve it by **cross multiplication**: if a/b = c/d then a x d = b x c.

**Example.** x / 5 = 6 / 10
Cross multiply: 10x = 30. So x = 3.

**Example (recipe).** 2 cups of flour for 3 cups of sugar. How much flour for 9 cups of sugar?
2 / 3 = f / 9. Cross multiply: 3f = 18. So f = 6 cups.

**Example (map scale).** 1 inch = 25 miles. A distance of 3.5 inches is 3.5 x 25 = 87.5 miles.

## Percent
Percent means "out of 100".
- Part = percent x whole. 25% of 80 = 0.25 x 80 = 20.
- Percent = part / whole. 15 is what percent of 60? 15 / 60 = 0.25 = 25%.
- Whole = part / percent. 40% of what number is 18? 18 / 0.4 = 45.

## Percent change
Percent change = (new - old) / old x 100.
Price rises from $50 to $65: (65 - 50) / 50 = 0.30, which is a 30% increase.

## Discounts
A $40 shirt is 15% off. Pay 85% of the price: 0.85 x 40 = $34.

## Common mistakes
- Dividing by the new value instead of the original when finding percent change.
- Setting up a proportion upside down (flour over sugar on one side, sugar over flour on the other).
- Forgetting to convert a percent to a decimal before multiplying.
