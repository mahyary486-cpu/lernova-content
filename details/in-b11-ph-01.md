# Early experiments, site and pigments

Priestley, Ingenhousz (light needed), Sachs (starch), Engelmann (action spectrum) and van Niel (oxygen from water). Photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts; chlorophyll a is the main pigment and chlorophyll b and carotenoids are accessory pigments.

The overall reaction is 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.
