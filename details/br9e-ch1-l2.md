# Present Perfect

## Formação

**have/has + particípio passado**: *I have seen*, *she has lived*, *they have finished*.

## Usos principais

- **Experiências de vida:** Have you ever been to Brazil?
- **Ações recentes, com ligação ao presente:** He has just arrived.
- **Situações que continuam:** She has lived in Rio for five years.

## For e since

- **for** + período: *for ten years*, *for two hours*;
- **since** + ponto de partida: *since 2018*, *since she was born*.

## Particípios irregulares

see → seen, write → written, eat → eaten, go → gone/been.

## Cuidado

Com tempo passado definido usa-se o Simple Past: *I visited Rome last year* (e não *have visited ... last year*).

Negativa: *She hasn't finished her report yet.*
