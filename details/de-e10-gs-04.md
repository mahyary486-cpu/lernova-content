# Relativsätze (Relative Clauses)

## Relativpronomen
who (Personen), which (Sachen), that (Personen und Sachen, nur defining), whose (Besitz), where (Ort), when, why.

## Defining
Notwendige Information, ohne Kommas: *The man who lives next door is a doctor. The book which I bought is great.* Das Pronomen kann als Objekt wegfallen.

## Non-defining
Zusatzinformation, mit Kommas, nie that: *The woman, who is my aunt, lives in Berlin. The film, which I saw last week, was great.*

## which für den ganzen Satz
*He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.*
