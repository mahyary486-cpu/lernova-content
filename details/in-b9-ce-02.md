# Cell organelles

- Plasma membrane: selectively permeable, made of lipids and proteins
- Nucleus: control centre with DNA in chromosomes
- Cytoplasm: jelly-like fluid
- Mitochondria: release energy as ATP (powerhouse)
- Ribosomes: make proteins
- Endoplasmic reticulum: rough (ribosomes) and smooth (lipids, detox)
- Golgi apparatus: modifies, packages and dispatches
- Lysosomes: digestive enzymes
- Vacuoles: storage

Plastids (chloroplasts, chromoplasts, leucoplasts) are found in plants.
