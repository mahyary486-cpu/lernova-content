# Hydrocarbons, homologous series and nomenclature

Saturated hydrocarbons (alkanes, CₙH₂ₙ₊₂) have single bonds; unsaturated hydrocarbons are alkenes (CₙH₂ₙ) and alkynes (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂). A homologous series has the same functional group and successive members differ by CH₂.

Functional groups: –OH (alcohol), –CHO (aldehyde), >C=O (ketone), –COOH (carboxylic acid). Isomers have the same formula but different structures.
