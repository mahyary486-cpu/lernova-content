# Force and pressure

Pressure is the amount of force that pushes on each unit of surface area.

## Key ideas

- A force is a push or a pull. It is measured in newtons (N). The weight of an object is the force of gravity on it: W = m × g. Near Earth's surface g ≈ 10 N/kg, so a 1 kg object weighs about 10 N.
- A force that acts on a surface and is perpendicular to it is called a normal force. Pressure depends on this force, not on the object's mass directly.
- Pressure (P) is the normal force (F) divided by the area (A) on which it acts: P = F / A.
- The SI unit of pressure is the pascal (Pa). 1 Pa = 1 N/m^2. This is a very small pressure, so people also use kilopascals: 1 kPa = 1000 Pa.
- For the same force, a smaller area gives a larger pressure, and a larger area gives a smaller pressure.
- For the same area, a larger force gives a larger pressure.
- From the formula we can also find the other quantities: F = P × A and A = F / P.
- Always convert area to square metres before you calculate. For example, 1 cm^2 = 0.0001 m^2 and 1 m^2 = 10 000 cm^2.

Imagine pressing your thumb and then the sharp tip of a pin against a wall with the same push. The pin makes a much bigger pressure because its tip has a tiny area. Your thumb only feels a soft push because the force is spread over a large area.

## Examples

**Example 1: A box on the floor.** A box weighs 600 N. Its bottom face has an area of 0.5 m^2. Find the pressure on the floor.
- Step 1: F = 600 N, A = 0.5 m^2.
- Step 2: P = F / A = 600 / 0.5.
- Step 3: P = 1200 Pa.

**Example 2: A person standing.** A person has a weight of 500 N. The soles of both shoes touch the ground with a total area of 0.04 m^2. Each shoe has an area of 0.02 m^2. Find the pressure on two feet and on one foot.
- Step 1: On two feet, P = 500 / 0.04 = 12 500 Pa.
- Step 2: On one foot, the area is only 0.02 m^2, so P = 500 / 0.02 = 25 000 Pa.
- Step 3: The pressure doubles because the area is halved while the force stays the same.

**Example 3: Finding the force.** A pressure of 50 000 Pa acts on a surface of area 0.002 m^2. Find the force.
- Step 1: F = P × A.
- Step 2: F = 50 000 × 0.002.
- Step 3: F = 100 N.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing up force and pressure. Force is measured in N, pressure is measured in Pa. They are not the same thing.
- Forgetting to convert cm^2 to m^2 before calculating. This gives an answer that is wrong by a factor of 10 000.
- Thinking that a heavier object always makes a larger pressure. A heavy object on a very large area can make a smaller pressure than a light object on a tiny area.
- Using mass in kilograms instead of weight in newtons in the formula. First find the weight with W = m × g.

## Remember

- P = F / A, and 1 Pa = 1 N/m^2.
- Same force with a smaller area makes a larger pressure.
- Convert all areas to m^2 and all forces to N before you calculate.
