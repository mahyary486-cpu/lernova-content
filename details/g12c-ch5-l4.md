# Atmospheric Chemistry: Pollution, Greenhouse Effect and Ozone

Cars, power stations and factories release gases that cause smog, acid rain and global warming.

## Key ideas
- **CO** binds to hemoglobin; **NOx** forms at high engine temperatures.
- **SO2 + H2O -> H2SO3**: **acid rain**.
- **Greenhouse gases**: CO2, CH4, N2O, H2O vapor absorb **infrared**.
- **Ozone layer** absorbs **ultraviolet**.
- **CFCs** release **Cl atoms** that destroy ozone.
- A **catalytic converter** turns CO, NOx, hydrocarbons into **CO2, N2, H2O**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Acid rain.** Write the reaction of sulfur trioxide with water.
1. SO3 combines with water.
2. SO3 + H2O -> H2SO4 (sulfuric acid).

**Example 2: Combustion.** How many moles of CO2 are produced by burning 1 mol of octane C8H18?
1. C8H18 + 12.5 O2 -> 8 CO2 + 9 H2O.
2. 8 mol of CO2.

**Example 3: Ozone.** Why do CFCs damage the ozone layer?
1. UV light splits CFCs and releases Cl atoms.
2. Each Cl atom catalyzes the destruction of many ozone molecules.

## Common mistakes
- Confusing the greenhouse effect with the ozone hole.
- Thinking CO2 causes acid rain mainly.
- Forgetting that Cl acts as a catalyst.
