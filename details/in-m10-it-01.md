# Trigonometric ratios of an acute angle

In a right triangle, for an acute angle A: sin A = opposite/hypotenuse, cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan A = opposite/adjacent. Their reciprocals are cosec A, sec A and cot A, and tan A = sin A / cos A.

Example: if sin A = 3/5 then cos A = 4/5 and tan A = 3/4.
