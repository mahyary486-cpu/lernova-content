# Respiratory quotient and the amphibolic pathway

RQ = CO₂ released / O₂ consumed: 1 for carbohydrates, about 0.7 for fats and 0.9 for proteins, and infinite in anaerobic respiration. The Krebs cycle is amphibolic: it is catabolic and gives intermediates for synthesis.

Fats enter as acetyl CoA after β-oxidation.
