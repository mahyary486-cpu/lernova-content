# Streamline flow and Bernoulli's principle

For incompressible streamline flow A₁v₁ = A₂v₂. Bernoulli's equation: P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant, so where the speed is high the pressure is low. Applications include the venturimeter, aeroplane lift and Torricelli's speed √(2gh).

The Reynolds number decides whether flow is laminar or turbulent.
