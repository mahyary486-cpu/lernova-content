# Torque and angular momentum

Torque τ = r × F and angular momentum L = r × p; τ = dL/dt. With no external torque L is conserved, so a dancer spins faster when she pulls her arms in. A couple has zero net force and torque F d.

Example: a 10 N force at 2 m at 30° to the arm gives a torque of 10 N m.
