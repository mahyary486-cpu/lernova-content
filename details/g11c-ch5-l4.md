# Chemical Equilibrium and the Equilibrium Constant

At equilibrium the reaction has not stopped. Forward and reverse reactions continue at the same rate.

## Key ideas
- **Dynamic equilibrium**: forward rate = reverse rate.
- **K = [products]^coefficients / [reactants]^coefficients**.
- Leave out **pure solids and liquids**.
- **K > 1**: products favored; **K < 1**: reactants favored.
- K changes **only with temperature**.
- **Q < K**: forward; **Q > K**: reverse; **Q = K**: equilibrium.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Writing K.** Write K for N2 + 3 H2 <-> 2 NH3.
1. Products over reactants with the coefficients as powers.
2. K = [NH3]^2 / ([N2][H2]^3).

**Example 2: Calculating K.** At equilibrium [N2O4] = 0.40 M and [NO2] = 0.20 M for N2O4 <-> 2 NO2.
1. K = [NO2]^2 / [N2O4] = 0.04 / 0.40.
2. K = 0.10.

**Example 3: Reaction quotient.** For the same reaction K = 0.10 and Q = 0.50.
1. Q is greater than K.
2. The reaction shifts to the left (reverse).

## Common mistakes
- Including solids and liquids in K.
- Forgetting the powers from the coefficients.
- Thinking a catalyst changes K.
