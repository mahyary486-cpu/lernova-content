# Linear polynomials in real life

Many situations have a fixed part plus a part that grows steadily. A taxi that charges ₹50 plus ₹12 per km has the fare 12x + 50 for x km; for 10 km the fare is ₹170.

To find the line through two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2), compute the slope (y2 − y1)/(x2 − x1) and then the intercept. Two lines meet where their polynomials are equal: y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 4 meet at (1, 3).
