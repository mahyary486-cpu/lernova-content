# 主语从句和表语从句

名词性从句在句中起**名词**的作用，包括主语从句、表语从句、宾语从句和同位语从句。

## 主语从句

在句中作**主语**。

- **That** he is late is true.
- **What** he said made everyone angry.
- **Whether** he will come is not clear.

**形式主语 it：** 主语从句较长时，用 it 作形式主语，把从句放到后面：

- **It** is a pity **that** he can't come.
- **It** is said **that** he has gone abroad.
- **It** doesn't matter **whether** he comes.

## 表语从句

在句中作**表语**，放在系动词后。

- The question is **whether** we should start now.
- My advice is **that** you should take more exercise.
- This is **why** he was late.（This is the reason ...）

## 注意

- **The reason is that ...**，不用 because：
  - ✅ The reason is **that** he missed the bus.
  - ❌ The reason is because he missed the bus.
- 主语从句和表语从句中，不用 if，**用 whether**。
