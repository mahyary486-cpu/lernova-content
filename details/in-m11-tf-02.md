# Trigonometric functions and their signs

For a real x: sin x, cos x, tan x with the reciprocals cosec, sec, cot. sin²x + cos²x = 1, 1 + tan²x = sec²x and 1 + cot²x = cosec²x. sin x and cos x have period 2π and range [−1, 1]; tan x has period π.

In quadrants I, II, III, IV: all, sin, tan, cos are positive respectively.
