# The mole concept and Avogadro number

One mole contains 6.022 × 10²³ particles (the Avogadro number). The mass of one mole is the molar mass, in g/mol, and equals the atomic or molecular mass in grams.

Moles = mass / molar mass = number of particles / 6.022 × 10²³. For example 36 g of water is 2 mol, and 11 g of CO₂ is 0.25 mol.
