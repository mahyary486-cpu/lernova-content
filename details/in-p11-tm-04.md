# Second law, heat engines and Carnot engine

Kelvin–Planck: no engine can convert heat completely into work. Clausius: heat cannot flow from cold to hot by itself. Efficiency η = W/Q₁ = 1 − Q₂/Q₁; the Carnot engine has η = 1 − T₂/T₁, the maximum possible.

A refrigerator has a coefficient of performance Q₂/W = T₂/(T₁ − T₂). Entropy of an isolated system never decreases.
