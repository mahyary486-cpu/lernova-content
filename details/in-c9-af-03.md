# Atomic and molecular mass, ions and chemical formulae

Atomic mass is measured in u, where 1 u is 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. Molecular mass is the sum of atomic masses, for example H₂O = 18 u and CO₂ = 44 u.

Ions are charged particles: cations are positive and anions negative. Formulae are written by crossing over valencies, for example Al₂O₃, CaCl₂ and (NH₄)₂SO₄.
