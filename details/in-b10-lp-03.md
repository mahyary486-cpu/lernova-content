# Transportation

The human heart has four chambers and double circulation: deoxygenated blood goes to the lungs through the pulmonary artery and oxygenated blood is pumped to the body through the aorta. Arteries carry blood away from the heart, veins carry it back and capillaries exchange materials.

In plants, xylem carries water and minerals, and phloem translocates food. Transpiration pulls water up.
