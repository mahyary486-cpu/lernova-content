# Important salts and their uses

Common salt (NaCl) gives NaOH, Cl₂ and H₂ by the chlor-alkali process. Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) is used to disinfect water. Baking soda (NaHCO₃) is used in baking and as an antacid. Washing soda is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.

Plaster of Paris is CaSO₄·½H₂O, made by heating gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) and used to set fractured bones.
