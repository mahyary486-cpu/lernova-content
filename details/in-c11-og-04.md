# Purification and analysis

Purification: crystallisation, sublimation, distillation, steam distillation, differential extraction and chromatography. Lassaigne's test detects N (Prussian blue), S (violet with nitroprusside) and halogens. Liebig's method estimates C and H as CO₂ and H₂O; Dumas and Kjeldahl methods estimate nitrogen.

Kjeldahl's method fails for nitro and azo compounds.
