# Condensation Polymers

Clothes made of polyester or nylon are condensation polymers, built with release of water at every link.

## Key ideas
- **Condensation**: monomers join with loss of a **small molecule** (H2O).
- **Polyester**: acid + **diol** -> **ester** link.
- **Polyamide (nylon)**: acid + **diamine** -> **amide** link.
- Each monomer has **two functional groups**.
- Can be broken by **hydrolysis**.
- Natural: **proteins**, **starch**, **cellulose**, **DNA**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Water released.** How many H2O molecules are released when a polyester of 100 monomer units (50 acid + 50 diol) forms?
1. There are 99 links for 100 units in a chain.
2. 99 molecules of water (about 100).

**Example 2: Monomers of nylon.** What monomers form nylon-6,6?
1. The polymer has amide links.
2. Hexanedioic acid and 1,6-diaminohexane.

**Example 3: Link type.** Which link is formed between an amino acid and another amino acid?
1. The amine of one reacts with the acid of the other.
2. A peptide (amide) link.

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting that the small molecule is lost.
- Mixing polyester and polyamide links.
- Using monomers with only one functional group.
