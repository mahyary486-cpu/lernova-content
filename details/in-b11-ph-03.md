# Dark reaction: C₃ and C₄

The Calvin cycle in the stroma has carboxylation by RuBisCO (CO₂ + RuBP → 3-PGA), reduction and regeneration; it needs 3 ATP and 2 NADPH per CO₂. The C₄ pathway (Hatch and Slack) forms OAA from PEP in mesophyll cells and has Kranz anatomy.

Maize and sugarcane are C₄ plants.
