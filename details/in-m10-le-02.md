# Consistency and the ratio conditions

If a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂, the lines intersect and the pair is consistent with a unique solution. If a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂, the lines are parallel and the pair is inconsistent. If a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂, the lines coincide and there are infinitely many solutions (dependent).

Example: 2x + 3y = 7 and 4x + 6y = 14 have equal ratios 1/2, so the pair is dependent.
