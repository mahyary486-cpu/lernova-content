# Percent, discounts and sales tax

**Percent** means "per hundred". 25 % of 80 is 0.25 × 80 = 20.

**Discount.** A $60 jacket that is 25 % off has sale price 60 × 0.75 = $45.

**Sales tax.** In Ontario the HST is 13 %. The tax on $50 is 0.13 × 50 = $6.50, and the total is $56.50.

**Percent change.** If a price increases by 20 % and then decreases by 20 %, the final price is 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 of the original, which is a **4 % decrease**.

**Tip.** A 15 % tip on a $40 meal is 0.15 × 40 = $6.
