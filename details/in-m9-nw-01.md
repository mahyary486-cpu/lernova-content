# From natural numbers to real numbers

Natural numbers are 1, 2, 3, ...; whole numbers add 0; integers add the negatives. Rational numbers are numbers of the form p/q with integers p and q (q ≠ 0). Every integer is rational.

Numbers that cannot be written as p/q are irrational, such as √2 and π. Rational and irrational numbers together make up the real numbers, and every real number has a place on the number line.
