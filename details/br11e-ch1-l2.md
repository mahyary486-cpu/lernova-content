# Reported questions and commands

## Perguntas indiretas

A ordem é a de **afirmação** (sujeito + verbo), e há recuo dos tempos.

- *"Where do you live?"* → He asked me where I **lived**.
- *"What time is it?"* → He asked what time it **was**.
- *"Why are you crying?"* → She asked me why I **was** crying.
- *"Where have you been?"* → She asked me where I **had been**.

## Perguntas de sim ou não

Usa-se **if** ou **whether**:

- *"Do you like coffee?"* → She asked me **if** I liked coffee.
- *"Are you tired?"* → She asked **whether** I was tired.
- *"Can you help me?"* → She asked **if I could** help her.
- *"Will you come?"* → He asked if I **would** come.

## Ordens e pedidos

**told + objeto + to + verbo:**

- *"Open the door."* → He told me **to open** the door.
- *"Don't be late."* → She told us **not to be** late.
- *"Don't touch that!"* → He told the kids **not to touch** that.

*She **told** me to sit down.*
