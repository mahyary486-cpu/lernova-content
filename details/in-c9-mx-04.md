# Methods of separation

- Filtration: insoluble solid from liquid
- Evaporation: dissolved solid from liquid (salt from sea water)
- Distillation: miscible liquids with different boiling points
- Fractional distillation: crude oil, liquid air
- Separating funnel: immiscible liquids
- Magnet: iron from sand
- Sublimation: ammonium chloride or camphor
- Centrifugation: cream from milk
- Chromatography: coloured components of ink; Rf = distance moved by substance / distance moved by solvent

Choose the method from the properties of the components.
