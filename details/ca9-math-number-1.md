# Integers and rational numbers

**Integers** are whole numbers and their opposites: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …. **Rational numbers** can be written as a fraction a / b with b ≠ 0, for example 3/4 = 0.75.

**Operations with integers.**

- Adding a negative number moves left on the number line: −3 + 5 = 2.
- The product of two negatives is positive: (−4) × (−2) = 8.
- The product of a positive and a negative is negative.

**Order of operations (BEDMAS).** Brackets, Exponents, Division and Multiplication (left to right), Addition and Subtraction (left to right).

**Example.** 8 − 2 × (3 + 1)² = 8 − 2 × 16 = 8 − 32 = −24.
