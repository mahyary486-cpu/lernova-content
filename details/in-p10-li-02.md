# Mirror formula and magnification

With the new Cartesian sign convention, 1/v + 1/u = 1/f and magnification m = −v/u. A concave mirror forms a real inverted image except when the object is between P and F, where the image is virtual, erect and enlarged. A convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect, diminished image.

Example: f = −15 cm, u = −30 cm gives v = −30 cm and m = −1.
