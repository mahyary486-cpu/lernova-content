# Probability of an event

When all outcomes are equally likely, the **probability** of an event is

P = (number of favorable outcomes) / (total number of outcomes).

Always 0 ≤ P ≤ 1. A sure event has P = 1 and an impossible event has P = 0.

**Examples.**

- A fair coin gives heads with P = 1/2.
- A die shows 6 with P = 1/6, and an even number with P = 3/6 = 1/2.
- A bag with 3 red and 7 blue balls gives a red ball with P = 3/10.
- Two dice have 36 outcomes, and 6 of them give sum 7, so P = 6/36 = 1/6.
- From a 36-card deck, an ace has P = 4/36 = 1/9.

**At least one.** P(at least one head in two tosses) = 1 − P(no heads) = 1 − 1/4 = 3/4.
