# Simple machines and mechanical advantage

Simple machines (lever, pulley, inclined plane, wheel and axle) change the size or direction of a force; they do not create energy. Mechanical advantage MA = load / effort. Efficiency = useful output / input × 100%.

A lever pivots on a fulcrum. First-class: fulcrum between effort and load (seesaw). Second-class: load between fulcrum and effort (wheelbarrow). A fixed pulley has MA 1; a block and tackle with n supporting strands has an ideal MA of n. An inclined plane has an ideal MA of length / height.
