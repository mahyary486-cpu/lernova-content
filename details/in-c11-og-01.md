# Classification and nomenclature

Carbon is tetravalent and shows catenation. Functional groups: –OH alcohol, –CHO aldehyde, C=O ketone, –COOH carboxylic acid, –NH₂ amine. IUPAC names use a prefix for the chain length (meth, eth, prop, but) and a suffix for the group (-ol, -al, -one, -oic acid).

Example: CH₃COCH₃ is propanone.
