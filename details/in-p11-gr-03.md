# Gravitational potential energy and escape speed

The gravitational potential energy is U = −GMm/r and the potential is V = −GM/r. The escape speed is v_e = √(2gR) ≈ 11.2 km/s for the Earth, and the orbital speed near the surface is √(gR) ≈ 7.9 km/s, so v_e = √2 v₀.

Escape speed does not depend on the mass of the escaping body.
