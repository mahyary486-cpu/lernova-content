# Bohr's model and electron shells

Bohr proposed that electrons revolve in fixed orbits called energy levels or shells (K, L, M, N) and do not radiate energy while in them. Shells hold at most 2, 8, 18 and 32 electrons, and the outermost shell holds at most 8.

The outer electrons are valence electrons. Valency is the number of electrons lost, gained or shared to reach a full outer shell, for example sodium 2, 8, 1 has valency 1.
