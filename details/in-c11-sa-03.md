# Quantum mechanical model and quantum numbers

Matter has wave character (λ = h/mv) and the uncertainty principle limits knowledge of position and momentum. The quantum numbers are n (shell), l (shape, 0 to n − 1), m_l (orientation, −l to +l) and m_s (spin ±½).

Subshells s, p, d and f hold 2, 6, 10 and 14 electrons; a shell holds 2n².
