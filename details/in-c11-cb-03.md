# Hybridisation and valence bond theory

sp (linear, 180°), sp² (planar, 120°), sp³ (tetrahedral, 109.5°), sp³d (trigonal bipyramidal) and sp³d² (octahedral). Sigma bonds form by head-on overlap and pi bonds by sideways overlap: ethene has 5 sigma and 1 pi bond, ethyne has 2 pi bonds.

Diamond is sp³ and graphite is sp².
