# Atomic number, mass number, isotopes and isobars

The atomic number Z is the number of protons; the mass number A is protons plus neutrons. Neutrons = A − Z.

Isotopes are atoms of one element with the same Z but different A, for example protium and deuterium. They share chemical properties. Isobars have the same A but different Z, for example Ca-40 and Ar-40. Isotopes are used in nuclear fuel (U-235), cancer treatment (Co-60) and goitre treatment (iodine).
