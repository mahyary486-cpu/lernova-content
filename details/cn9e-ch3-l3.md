# 被动语态的用法与注意

## 不能用于被动语态的情况

1. **不及物动词**（没有宾语）：happen, take place, appear, die, rise
   - ✅ The accident **happened** last night.
   - ❌ The accident was happened last night.
2. **系动词**：look, sound, feel, taste, become
3. 有些动词表示被动含义时用主动形式：The book **sells** well.

## 感官动词和使役动词

主动句中感官动词（see, hear, watch, notice）和 make、let 后面跟**不带 to 的动词不定式**；变成被动句时，要**加上 to**。

- 主动：We saw him **cross** the road.
- 被动：He was seen **to cross** the road.
- 主动：The boss made them work all night.
- 被动：They were made **to work** all night.

## 带 by 的被动句

- Beethoven wrote the music. → The music was written **by Beethoven**.
- 动作的执行者重要时，加 by 短语。
