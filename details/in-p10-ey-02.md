# Defects of vision

Myopia (short-sightedness) is corrected with a concave lens; hypermetropia (long-sightedness) with a convex lens; presbyopia with bifocal lenses. A cataract is clouding of the eye lens.

Power of a corrective lens is P = 1/f in dioptres; a myopic eye with far point 50 cm needs −2 D.
