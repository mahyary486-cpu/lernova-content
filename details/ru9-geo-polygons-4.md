# Area of a circle and of a sector

The **area** of a circle of radius r is S = πr². For r = 4, S = 16π.

A **sector** of α degrees is the fraction α / 360 of the circle, so its area is

S = πr²α / 360.

**Examples.** A 60° sector of a circle of radius 6 has area π · 36 · 60 / 360 = 6π. If a circle has area 49π then r = 7, and an arc of 120° has length π · 7 · 120 / 180 = 14π / 3.

**Ring (annulus).** The area between circles of radii R and r is π(R² − r²). For R = 5 and r = 3 it is 25π − 9π = 16π.
