# Refraction of light

Refraction is the bending of light as it goes from one medium to another because its speed changes. Light bends towards the normal in a denser medium and away from the normal in a rarer medium.

Snell's law: sin i / sin r = n, the refractive index, and n = c / v. Glass (n = 1.5) has light speed 2 × 10⁸ m/s.
