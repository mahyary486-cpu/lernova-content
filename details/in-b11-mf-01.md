# The root, stem and leaf

Tap roots (dicots) develop from the radicle; fibrous roots (monocots) and adventitious roots arise from other parts. Roots may be modified (prop, stilt, pneumatophores, storage). The stem bears nodes and internodes and can be modified into tubers, tendrils and thorns.

Leaves have a leaf base, petiole and lamina, with reticulate (dicot) or parallel (monocot) venation; phyllotaxy is alternate, opposite or whorled.
