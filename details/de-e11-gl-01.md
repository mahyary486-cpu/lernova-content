# Gerund und Infinitiv

## Verb + -ing
enjoy, avoid, suggest, admit, mind, can't stand, can't help, look forward to, it's no use.

## Verb + to-Infinitiv
want, decide, hope, promise, refuse, manage, learn.

## Bedeutungsunterschiede
- remember doing (sich erinnern) / remember to do (nicht vergessen)
- stop doing (aufhören) / stop to do (anhalten, um etwas zu tun)
- try doing (ausprobieren) / try to do (versuchen)
- regret doing / regret to tell
- be used to + -ing (gewohnt) / used to + Infinitiv (früher)
