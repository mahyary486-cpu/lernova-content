# Superposition and standing waves

Superposition: the net displacement is the sum of the individual ones. Standing waves have nodes (zero displacement) separated by λ/2 and antinodes. A string fixed at both ends has fₙ = nv/2L; a closed pipe has odd harmonics (v/4L, 3v/4L, …); an open pipe has all harmonics (v/2L, …).

Two in-phase waves of amplitude A give 2A; in antiphase they cancel.
