# Plant and animal cells, prokaryotes and eukaryotes

Plant cells have a cellulose cell wall, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole. Animal cells have no cell wall or chloroplasts and may have centrioles for cell division.

Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) have no true nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles. Eukaryotic cells (plants, animals, fungi) have a nucleus enclosed by a membrane. Mitochondria have their own DNA.
