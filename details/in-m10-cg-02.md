# Applications of the distance formula

Use the distance formula to test collinearity (one distance equals the sum of the other two), to identify triangles (equilateral, isosceles, right-angled) and quadrilaterals (square, rhombus, parallelogram), and to find a point equidistant from two given points.

Example: (0, 0), (4, 0), (4, 4), (0, 4) are the vertices of a square because all sides are 4 and the diagonals are equal.
