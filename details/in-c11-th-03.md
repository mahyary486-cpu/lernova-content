# Types of enthalpy change

Standard enthalpy of formation is zero for elements in their standard state; combustion enthalpies are negative; bond dissociation enthalpies are positive. ΔH = Σ(bonds broken) − Σ(bonds formed). Neutralisation of a strong acid and base is about −57 kJ/mol.

Example: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl has ΔH = −183 kJ.
