# Reported statements

O **discurso indireto** relata o que alguém disse, sem citar as palavras exatas.

*Direto:* He said, "I am tired." → *Indireto:* He said that he **was** tired.

## Recuo dos tempos (backshift)

Depois de um verbo de relato no passado:

- present simple → past simple: *I like pizza* → she said she **liked** pizza;
- am/is/are → was/were;
- can → **could**; will → **would**;
- present perfect → **past perfect**: *We have finished* → they said they **had** finished;
- past perfect continua past perfect.

## Pronomes

*"I am happy"* → he said **he** was happy.

## Expressões de tempo

- today → **that day**;
- tomorrow → **the next day**;
- now → **then**.

## Estrutura

*said* é seguido de **that + oração**. *told* leva um objeto: *She told me that I was late.*
