# 被动语态进阶

## It is said that ... 句型

**It is said / reported / believed / thought / known that ...**

- **It is said that** he has gone abroad.

可以转换成：**主语 + be said / reported / believed + to do**

- He **is said to have gone** abroad.（完成式，说明动作发生在前）
- He **is said to be** a good teacher.

## 短语动词的被动语态

短语动词是一个整体，**不能拆开**。

- Her aunt **looked after** the baby. → The baby **was looked after** by her aunt.
- They **put off** the meeting. → The meeting **was put off**.

## need doing = need to be done

主语是物，表示"需要被……"：

- The room **needs cleaning**. = The room **needs to be cleaned**.
- The plants **need watering**.

## 不能用被动语态的动词

happen, take place, break out, appear, last, cost, fit

- ✅ The war **broke out** in 1939.
- ❌ The war was broken out in 1939.

## 提示

被动语态的 **by 短语**，只有执行者重要时才保留。
