# Trigonometric ratios of specific angles

Standard values: sin 0° = 0, sin 30° = 1/2, sin 45° = 1/√2, sin 60° = √3/2, sin 90° = 1. cos takes the same values in reverse order. tan 0° = 0, tan 30° = 1/√3, tan 45° = 1, tan 60° = √3 and tan 90° is undefined.

Example: sin 60° cos 30° + sin 30° cos 60° = 3/4 + 1/4 = 1.
