# Carbohydrates and Lipids

Plants store energy as starch, and animals store it as fat, which holds more than twice the energy per gram.

## Key ideas
- **Glucose** C6H12O6; **disaccharide** = 2 monosaccharides - H2O.
- **Starch**, **glycogen**, **cellulose** = polymers of glucose.
- **Fat** = glycerol + **3 fatty acids** (triester).
- **Saturated**: solid fats; **unsaturated**: liquid oils.
- **Hydrolysis** with NaOH gives **soap** (saponification).
- Fats: about **38 kJ/g**; carbohydrates: about **17 kJ/g**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Disaccharide.** Two glucose molecules (C6H12O6) form maltose. Find the formula.
1. 2 C6H12O6 = C12H24O12.
2. Minus one H2O gives C12H22O11.

**Example 2: Fat.** How many fatty acid chains are in a triglyceride?
1. Glycerol has three OH groups.
2. Three ester links, so three chains.

**Example 3: Energy.** Compare 10 g of fat and 10 g of carbohydrate (38 and 17 kJ/g).
1. Fat: 10 x 38 = 380 kJ; carbohydrate: 10 x 17 = 170 kJ.
2. Fat provides 210 kJ more.

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting the water lost in a glycosidic link.
- Thinking starch and cellulose are the same.
- Confusing saturated and unsaturated fats.
