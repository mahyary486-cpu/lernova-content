# 强调句

## 基本结构

**It is / was + 被强调部分 + that / who + 其余部分**

- 被强调的是**人**：可用 **who** 或 that；
- 被强调的是**物、时间、地点、原因**：用 **that**。

## 例句

原句：Tom broke the window yesterday.

- 强调主语：**It was Tom that / who** broke the window yesterday.
- 强调宾语：**It was the window that** Tom broke yesterday.
- 强调时间：**It was yesterday that** Tom broke the window.

## 一般疑问句和特殊疑问句

- 一般疑问句：**Was it Tom that** broke the window?
- 特殊疑问句：**Who was it that** broke the window?

## 强调谓语动词

用助动词 **do / does / did** + 动词原形：

- I **do** like the film.
- He **did** come to see you.

## 判断是不是强调句

把 **It is / was ... that** 去掉，句子仍然**完整**，就是强调句。

> It was in 2008 that the Olympics were held.（去掉后：The Olympics were held in 2008. 完整）
