# Lipids and nucleic acids

Fats are esters of glycerol and fatty acids (saturated or unsaturated); phospholipids form membranes. A nucleotide is a base, a pentose sugar and a phosphate; purines are A and G, pyrimidines C, T and U. DNA has deoxyribose, RNA ribose; A pairs with T (2 hydrogen bonds) and G with C (3).

Watson and Crick proposed the double helix.
