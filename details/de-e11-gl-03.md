# Inversion und Betonung

## Inversion
Nach negativen Adverbien am Satzanfang folgt die Frageform: *Never have I seen such a film. Rarely do you see ... Not only was he rude, but also late. Hardly had she arrived when it started to rain.*

## Konditionalsatz ohne if
*Were I you, I would accept. Should you need help, call me.*

## Cleft sentences
*It was Tom who broke the window. What I need is a holiday.*

## Wünsche
*I wish I had known. It's high time you went to bed. He behaves as if he were the boss.*
