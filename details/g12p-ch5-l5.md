# Fission, Fusion and the Standard Model

The Sun releases energy by fusing hydrogen into helium, and nuclear power stations release energy by splitting uranium.

## Key ideas
- **Fission**: heavy nucleus splits; **chain reaction**; about **200 MeV** per fission.
- **Fusion**: light nuclei join; powers the **Sun**.
- Energy is released because **binding energy per nucleon** increases toward **iron-56**.
- **Quarks** and **leptons** are the building blocks.
- **Bosons** carry forces: photon (EM), gluon (strong), W and Z (weak).
- **Matter + antimatter -> energy** (annihilation).

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Energy from fission.** How much energy is released by the fission of 1 mol of uranium-235 at 200 MeV per nucleus? (1 MeV = 1.6 x 10^-13 J, N_A = 6.02 x 10^23)
1. E per nucleus = 200 x 1.6 x 10^-13 = 3.2 x 10^-11 J.
2. E = 3.2 x 10^-11 x 6.02 x 10^23 = 1.9 x 10^13 J.

**Example 2: Mass-energy.** A fusion reaction releases 17.6 MeV. How much mass is converted?
1. delta m = E / c^2 = 17.6 / 931.5 u.
2. delta m = 0.0189 u.

**Example 3: Quarks.** A proton is made of two up quarks (+2/3 each) and one down quark (-1/3).
1. Total charge = 2 x 2/3 - 1/3.
2. Charge = +1.

## Common mistakes
- Thinking fusion works at room temperature.
- Mixing up fission and fusion.
- Forgetting that mass is converted into energy.
