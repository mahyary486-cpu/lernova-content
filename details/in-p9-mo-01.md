# Distance, displacement, speed and velocity

Distance is the total path length (scalar, metre). Displacement is the shortest straight-line change of position with direction (vector). A runner who completes a lap has covered a distance of one lap but has zero displacement.

Speed = distance / time (m/s). Velocity is speed in a stated direction. Average speed = total distance / total time. Conversion: 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h, so 72 km/h = 20 m/s.
