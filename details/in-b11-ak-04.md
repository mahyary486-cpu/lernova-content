# Chordata

Chordates have a notochord, a dorsal hollow nerve cord and pharyngeal gill slits. Vertebrates: Cyclostomata, Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous), Osteichthyes (bony), Amphibia (moist skin, 3-chambered heart), Reptilia (scales), Aves (feathers, 4-chambered heart) and Mammalia (mammary glands, hair).

Birds and mammals are homoiothermous.
