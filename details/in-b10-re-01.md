# Asexual reproduction

In asexual reproduction a single parent gives offspring that are genetically similar. Methods are binary fission (Amoeba), multiple fission (Plasmodium), budding (Hydra, yeast), fragmentation and regeneration (Planaria), spore formation (Rhizopus) and vegetative propagation (potato, Bryophyllum). Tissue culture grows plants from small pieces of tissue.

Vegetative propagation lets plants bear flowers and fruits earlier.
