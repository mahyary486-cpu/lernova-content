# Statistics: mean, median, mode, range

For a list of numbers:

- **Mean** = sum divided by the count. For 2, 4, 6 the mean is 4.
- **Median** = the middle value of the sorted list. For 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 it is 5. With an even count, take the mean of the two middle values: for 2, 4, 7, 9 it is (4 + 7) / 2 = 5.5.
- **Mode** = the most frequent value. For 2, 3, 3, 5, 7 it is 3.
- **Range** = largest minus smallest. For 3, 8, 1, 12, 6 it is 12 − 1 = 11.

The median is not affected by one extreme value, while the mean is.

If the mean of 5 numbers is 8, their sum is 40. If four of them are 6, 7, 9, 10 (sum 32), the fifth is 8.
