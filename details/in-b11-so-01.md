# Epithelial and connective tissue

Epithelial tissue covers surfaces and lines organs: simple (squamous, cuboidal, columnar, ciliated) or compound; cell junctions are tight, adhering and gap. Connective tissue is loose (areolar, adipose), dense (tendons, ligaments) or specialised (cartilage, bone, blood).

Tendons connect muscle to bone and ligaments connect bone to bone.
