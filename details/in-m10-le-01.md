# Pair of linear equations and the graphical method

A pair of linear equations in two variables is a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0 and a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0. Each equation is a straight line.

In the graphical method, the solution is the point where the lines meet. Intersecting lines give one solution, parallel lines give none, and coincident lines give infinitely many.
