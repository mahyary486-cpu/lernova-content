# Sum, difference and multiple angle formulas

sin(x ± y) = sin x cos y ± cos x sin y; cos(x ± y) = cos x cos y ∓ sin x sin y; tan(x ± y) = (tan x ± tan y)/(1 ∓ tan x tan y). sin 2x = 2 sin x cos x, cos 2x = cos²x − sin²x = 1 − 2sin²x = 2cos²x − 1, sin 3x = 3 sin x − 4 sin³x, cos 3x = 4 cos³x − 3 cos x.

Example: sin 75° = (√6 + √2)/4.
