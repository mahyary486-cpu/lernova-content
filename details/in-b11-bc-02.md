# Kingdom Monera

Monera include bacteria, cyanobacteria, mycoplasma and archaebacteria. Bacterial cell walls contain peptidoglycan; Gram-positive bacteria stain purple and Gram-negative pink. Cyanobacteria (Nostoc) fix nitrogen in heterocysts; mycoplasma lacks a cell wall.

Archaebacteria live in extreme habitats and methanogens produce methane.
