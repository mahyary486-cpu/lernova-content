# 不定式的用法

不定式 = **to + 动词原形**。

## 作宾语

**常接不定式的动词：**

want, hope, decide, plan, wish, refuse, agree, manage, afford, promise, expect, learn

- I **want to be** a doctor.
- He **decided to leave**.

## 作宾语补足语

**ask / tell / want / allow / advise / invite + sb + to do**

- The teacher **asked us to read** the passage.

## 作目的状语、结果状语

- He came **to help** us.（目的）
- He hurried to the station, **only to find** the train had left.（意想不到的结果）

## 省略 to 的不定式

1. 情态动词后：can, must, may, will + **do**；
2. **make / let / have** + sb + **do**：The teacher made us **read** again；
3. **see / hear / watch / notice** + sb + **do**（强调全过程）：I saw him **cross** the road；
4. had better **do**；why not **do**。

**注意**：这些句子变成被动语态时，**要加上 to**：He was made **to read** again。
