# Human ear, hearing range, ultrasound and infrasound

The pinna collects sound, which makes the eardrum vibrate. Small bones pass the vibrations to the cochlea, where nerve signals are sent to the brain. Humans hear 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz.

Infrasound is below 20 Hz and ultrasound above 20 000 Hz. Ultrasound is used in SONAR, medical imaging and by bats for echolocation.
