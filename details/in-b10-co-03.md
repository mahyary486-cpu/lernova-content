# Coordination in plants

Plants respond by tropisms: phototropism (towards light), geotropism (roots grow down), hydrotropism and thigmotropism (touch, tendrils). Plant hormones include auxin (cell elongation), gibberellin (stem growth), cytokinin (cell division), abscisic acid (inhibits growth) and ethylene (ripening).

Auxin collects on the shaded side of a shoot, which makes it bend towards light.
