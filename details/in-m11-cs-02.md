# Parabola

A parabola is the set of points equidistant from a fixed point (the focus) and a fixed line (the directrix); e = 1. For y² = 4ax: focus (a, 0), directrix x = −a, axis the x-axis, latus rectum 4a.

Similarly x² = 4ay has focus (0, a). The sign of the coefficient decides the direction it opens.
