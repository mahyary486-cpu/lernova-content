# Circle

A circle is the set of points at a fixed distance r from a fixed point (the centre). With centre (h, k): (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r². The general form x² + y² + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0 has centre (−g, −f) and radius √(g² + f² − c).

Example: the centre and radius of x² + y² − 6x + 4y − 12 = 0 are (3, −2) and 5.
