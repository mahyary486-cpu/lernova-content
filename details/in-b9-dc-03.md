# Classification of plants and animals

Plants are divided into thallophytes (algae), bryophytes (mosses), pteridophytes (ferns), gymnosperms (naked seeds) and angiosperms (flowering, monocots and dicots).

Animals include Porifera, Cnidaria, Platyhelminthes, Nematoda, Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata and Chordata; vertebrates are fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals.
