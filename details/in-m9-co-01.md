# The Cartesian plane

Two number lines that cross at right angles form the Cartesian plane. The horizontal line is the x-axis, the vertical line is the y-axis, and they meet at the origin O(0, 0).

Every point is described by an ordered pair (x, y). The first number, the abscissa, is the distance along the x-axis; the second, the ordinate, is the distance along the y-axis. The order matters: (2, 5) and (5, 2) are different points.
