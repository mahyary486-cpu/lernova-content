# Work and power

Work is done when a force moves an object: W = F × s, in joules (1 J = 1 N · m). No displacement means no work, and a force perpendicular to the displacement does no work.

Power is the rate of doing work: P = W / t, in watts (1 W = 1 J/s, 1 kW = 1000 W). Energy used is power × time; 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J.
