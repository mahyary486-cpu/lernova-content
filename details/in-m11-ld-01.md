# Limits and algebra of limits

The limit of f(x) as x → a is the value f(x) approaches as x gets close to a. Limit laws: the limit of a sum, difference, product and quotient is the sum, difference, product and quotient of the limits (denominator limit not 0).

Example: lim (x → 2) (x² − 4)/(x − 2) = lim (x + 2) = 4.
