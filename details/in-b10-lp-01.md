# Nutrition

Autotrophs make food by photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ in the presence of light and chlorophyll, with CO₂ entering through stomata. Heterotrophs depend on other organisms.

In humans digestion begins in the mouth (salivary amylase), continues in the stomach (HCl, pepsin) and small intestine (bile, pancreatic juice), where absorption takes place in the villi.
