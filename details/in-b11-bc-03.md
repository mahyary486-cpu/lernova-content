# Kingdom Protista and Fungi

Protista are unicellular eukaryotes: chrysophytes (diatoms), dinoflagellates (red tides), Euglena, slime moulds and protozoans (Amoeba, Paramecium, Plasmodium). Fungi are heterotrophic with chitin cell walls and mycelium; classes: Phycomycetes (Rhizopus), Ascomycetes (Penicillium), Basidiomycetes (Agaricus) and Deuteromycetes.

Fungi are saprophytic, parasitic or symbiotic.
