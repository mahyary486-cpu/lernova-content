# Tissues

Meristematic tissues divide actively: apical (length) and lateral (girth). Permanent tissues are simple (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma) or complex (xylem and phloem). Xylem has tracheids, vessels, fibres and parenchyma; phloem has sieve tubes, companion cells, parenchyma and fibres.

Sclerenchyma cells are dead and lignified.
