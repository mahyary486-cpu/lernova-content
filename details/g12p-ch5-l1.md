# Special Relativity

At speeds close to the speed of light time and length are not absolute, and mass and energy are linked by E = m c^2.

## Key ideas
- **Postulates**: same physics in all inertial frames; **c is constant**.
- **gamma = 1 / sqrt(1 - v^2 / c^2)**.
- **Time dilation**: t = gamma t0; **length contraction**: L = L0 / gamma.
- **E = m c^2**; **E_total = gamma m c^2**; **KE = (gamma - 1) m c^2**.
- Nothing with mass can reach **c**.
- Evidence: **muon decay**, **GPS** corrections.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Gamma.** A spaceship moves at 0.6 c.
1. gamma = 1 / sqrt(1 - 0.36) = 1 / sqrt(0.64).
2. gamma = 1 / 0.8 = 1.25.

**Example 2: Time dilation.** A trip takes 10 years on Earth's clock at 0.6 c. How long does it take on the ship?
1. t0 = t / gamma = 10 / 1.25.
2. t0 = 8 years.

**Example 3: Rest energy.** Find the rest energy of 1 g of mass.
1. E = m c^2 = 0.001 x (3 x 10^8)^2.
2. E = 9 x 10^13 J.

## Common mistakes
- Mixing up proper time and dilated time.
- Using v / c in percent.
- Thinking mass is only converted in nuclear reactions.
