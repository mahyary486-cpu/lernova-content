# Das Passiv (Passive Voice)

## Bildung
Form von be + Partizip. Das Objekt des Aktivsatzes wird Subjekt: *The cat eats the mouse. → The mouse is eaten by the cat.*

## Zeiten
Simple Present: *English is spoken here.* Simple Past: *The window was broken.* Present Perfect: *The bridge has been built.* Modal: *The homework must be handed in.*

## Fragen
*When was the house built?*

## Persönliches Passiv
*I was given a book.* *It is said that he is rich. → He is said to be rich.*

Das Passiv benutzt man, wenn der Handelnde unwichtig oder unbekannt ist.
