# Chemical equilibrium and the equilibrium constant

Equilibrium is dynamic: forward and backward rates are equal. For aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD, Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ/[A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ and Kp = Kc(RT)^Δn. K depends only on temperature. If Q > K the reaction goes backward and if Q < K forward.

A catalyst does not change the position of equilibrium.
