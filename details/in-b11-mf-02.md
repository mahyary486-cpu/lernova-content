# Inflorescence and flower

The inflorescence is the arrangement of flowers on the floral axis. A flower has four whorls: calyx (sepals), corolla (petals), androecium (stamens) and gynoecium (carpels with stigma, style and ovary).

Aestivation (valvate, twisted, imbricate) and placentation (marginal, axile, parietal, free central, basal) are floral characters.
