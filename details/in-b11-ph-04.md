# Photorespiration and limiting factors

Photorespiration is the oxygenase activity of RuBisCO in C₃ plants; C₄ plants avoid it. Blackman's law of limiting factors: light, CO₂, temperature and water; optimum temperature is 20–25 °C for C₃ and 30–45 °C for C₄ plants.

CAM plants open stomata at night.
