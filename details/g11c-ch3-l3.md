# Colligative Properties: Vapor Pressure and Boiling and Freezing Points

Salt spread on an icy road lowers the freezing point of water, so the ice melts even below 0 degrees C.

## Key ideas
- Colligative properties depend on the **number of particles**.
- **Raoult**: **P = X_solvent P0**.
- **delta T_b = i K_b m**; **delta T_f = i K_f m**.
- Water: **K_b = 0.512**, **K_f = 1.86**.
- **van't Hoff i**: sugar 1, NaCl 2, CaCl2 3.
- Uses: road **salt**, **antifreeze**, cooking.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Freezing point.** A 1.0 m sugar solution in water.
1. delta T_f = i K_f m = 1 x 1.86 x 1.0.
2. The freezing point is -1.86 C.

**Example 2: Salt solution.** A 1.0 m NaCl solution (i = 2).
1. delta T_f = 2 x 1.86 x 1.0 = 3.72 C.
2. The freezing point is -3.72 C.

**Example 3: Boiling point.** A 2.0 m sugar solution.
1. delta T_b = 1 x 0.512 x 2.0 = 1.02 C.
2. The boiling point is 101.02 C.

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting the van't Hoff factor.
- Using molarity instead of molality.
- Adding delta T to the freezing point instead of subtracting.
