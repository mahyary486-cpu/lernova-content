# Solids of revolution

A solid of revolution is the solid made when a flat shape turns one full turn around a straight line called the axis.

## Key ideas

- The axis is fixed. Every point of the shape moves in a circle around it.
- Rectangle turned about one of its sides: a cylinder. The side on the axis gives the height, and the other side gives the radius.
- Right triangle turned about one of its legs: a cone. The leg on the axis is the height, the other leg is the radius, and the hypotenuse is the slant height.
- Semicircle turned about its diameter: a sphere. Its radius is the radius of the semicircle.
- Turning the same shape around a different axis can give a different solid.
- Once you know the solid, use the formulas for volume and surface area. Cylinder: V = πr^2h. Cone: V = 1/3 πr^2h. Sphere: V = 4/3 πr^3.
- A cross section through the axis of a solid of revolution shows the original shape twice, as a mirror image.

## Worked examples

Example 1. A rectangle with sides 3 cm and 5 cm turns about the side of length 5 cm. Find the volume of the solid.
Step 1: The solid is a cylinder. The side on the axis is the height, so h = 5 cm.
Step 2: The other side is the radius, so r = 3 cm.
Step 3: V = π × 3^2 × 5 = 45π cm^3.

Example 2. A right triangle has legs 3 cm and 4 cm. Compare the volumes of the cones made by turning it about each leg.
Step 1: About the leg of length 4: r = 3 and h = 4, so V = 1/3 × π × 9 × 4 = 12π cm^3.
Step 2: About the leg of length 3: r = 4 and h = 3, so V = 1/3 × π × 16 × 3 = 16π cm^3.
Step 3: The hypotenuse is 5 cm, because 3^2 + 4^2 = 25. It is the slant height in both cones.
Step 4: The second cone is larger. 16π is more than 12π.

Example 3. A semicircle with radius 6 cm turns about its diameter. Find the surface area and the volume of the solid.
Step 1: The solid is a sphere with r = 6 cm.
Step 2: S = 4 × π × 6^2 = 4 × 36π = 144π cm^2.
Step 3: V = 4/3 × π × 6^3 = 4/3 × 216π = 288π cm^3.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing up the radius and the height. The radius is the distance from the axis, and the height lies along the axis.
- Using a leg as the slant height. The slant height of the cone is the hypotenuse.
- Assuming that turning a triangle about different legs gives the same volume.
- Turning the shape only half a turn. The shape must turn a full turn to make the whole solid.

## Remember

- Rectangle gives a cylinder, right triangle gives a cone, semicircle gives a sphere.
- Find r and h from the shape and the axis, then use the formulas.
