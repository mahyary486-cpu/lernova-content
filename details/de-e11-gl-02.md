# Partizipialkonstruktionen

## Present Participle
*Walking down the street, I met an old friend.* = While I was walking ... Auch Grund: *Not knowing the answer, she kept silent.*

## Perfect Participle
*Having finished his work, he went home.* = Vorzeitigkeit.

## Reduzierte Relativsätze
*The man standing at the door is my uncle.* (aktiv) *The book written by Orwell is famous.* (passiv)
