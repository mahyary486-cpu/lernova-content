# Equations of motion

For uniform acceleration:

- v = u + a t
- s = u t + ½ a t²
- v² = u² + 2 a s

Free fall: a = g ≈ 9.8 m/s² (10 m/s² for easy calculation). A stone dropped from rest has v = g t and falls s = ½ g t²; after 3 s, v = 30 m/s and s = 45 m. A stone thrown upward at 20 m/s rises for 2 s to a height of 20 m.
