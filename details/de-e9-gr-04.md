# Modalverben und ihre Ersatzformen

## Wichtige Modalverben
- can / could: Fähigkeit (*She could swim when she was five.*)
- must: Pflicht; mustn't: Verbot (*You mustn't smoke here.*)
- have to: äußere Pflicht; don't have to: nicht nötig (*You don't have to come.*)
- may / might: Möglichkeit, Erlaubnis
- should: Rat; should have + Partizip: Vorwurf (*You should have called me.*)

## Schlussfolgerung
must be: sicher, can't be: unmöglich (*It can't be Tom. He is in Spain.*)

## Ersatzformen
Für fehlende Zeiten benutzt man be able to, have to: *I wasn't able to come.*
