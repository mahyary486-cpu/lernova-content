# 动名词的用法

动名词 = **动词 + -ing**，具有名词的功能。

## 作主语

- **Reading** is my hobby.
- **It's no use crying** over spilt milk.（形式主语 it）

## 作宾语

**只能接动名词的动词：**

enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, consider, practise, keep, miss, admit, imagine, risk

- He **enjoys playing** football.
- Would you **mind opening** the window?
- She **suggested going** to the cinema.

**介词后用动名词：**

- look forward to **seeing** you；be good at **singing**；thank you for **helping**。

## 常用固定结构

- can't help **doing**：忍不住做；
- be worth **doing**：值得做；
- It's no use / no good **doing**：做……没用；
- feel like **doing**：想要做；
- spend time (in) **doing**：花时间做；
- have difficulty (in) **doing**：做……有困难。
