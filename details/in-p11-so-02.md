# Hooke's law and elastic moduli

Within the elastic limit stress ∝ strain. Young's modulus Y = longitudinal stress / strain; shear modulus G = shear stress / shear strain; bulk modulus B = −P/(ΔV/V). For a wire e = FL/(AY) and the spring constant is k = AY/L.

Liquids have only a bulk modulus. Poisson's ratio is lateral strain over longitudinal strain.
