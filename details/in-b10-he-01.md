# Variation and Mendel's monohybrid cross

Variation is the differences among individuals; heredity is the passing of traits. Mendel crossed tall (TT) and dwarf (tt) pea plants: F₁ was all tall (Tt), and F₂ gave tall and dwarf in a 3 : 1 ratio and genotypes 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt.

The trait that shows in F₁ is dominant; the hidden one is recessive.
