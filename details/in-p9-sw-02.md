# Characteristics of sound waves

Amplitude is the maximum displacement of particles from their mean position; it decides loudness. Frequency f is the number of vibrations per second (hertz) and decides pitch. Time period T = 1/f. Wavelength λ is the distance between two consecutive compressions or rarefactions.

The wave speed is v = f × λ. For a given medium v is fixed, so a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength.
