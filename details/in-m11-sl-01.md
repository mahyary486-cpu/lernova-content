# Slope of a line

The slope of a line with inclination θ is m = tan θ. Through (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) it is m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁). The x-axis has slope 0 and a vertical line has undefined slope.

Parallel lines have equal slopes and perpendicular lines satisfy m₁m₂ = −1; the angle between lines satisfies tan θ = ∣(m₂ − m₁)/(1 + m₁m₂)∣.
