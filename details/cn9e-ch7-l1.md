# 时间与条件状语从句

## 时间状语从句

引导词：**when, while, as soon as, before, after, until, since**

- I'll call you **as soon as** I get there.
- **While** I was cooking, my mother came in.
- He didn't go to bed **until** his mother came back.（直到……才）

**not ... until ...**：直到……才……

## 条件状语从句

引导词：**if**（如果），**unless**（除非，如果不）

- **If** you study hard, you will pass the exam.
- **Unless** you hurry, you'll be late.（= If you don't hurry, you'll be late.）

## 主将从现

在时间和条件状语从句中：

- **主句**用一般将来时（或祈使句、含情态动词）；
- **从句**用**一般现在时**表示将来。

| ✅ 正确 | ❌ 错误 |
|---|---|
| I'll call you as soon as I **get** there. | I'll call you as soon as I will get there. |
| If it **rains**, we won't go. | If it will rain, we won't go. |

## 注意

- **unless** 本身有否定意思，不能再用 not：Unless you hurry ... ✅；Unless you don't hurry ... ❌。
