# Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, HCF and LCM

Every composite number can be written as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique apart from order. HCF takes the smallest power of each common prime; LCM takes the greatest power of every prime.

For two positive integers, HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b. Example: 72 = 2³ × 3² and 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5, so HCF = 24 and LCM = 360.
