# Cockroach and frog

Cockroach (Periplaneta americana): head, thorax and abdomen, chitinous exoskeleton, open circulation, tracheal respiration, Malpighian tubules, mosaic vision. Frog (Rana tigrina): moist skin, 3-chambered heart, respiration through lungs, skin and buccal cavity, kidneys, ureotelic, metamorphosis.

Both are poikilothermous (frog) or uricotelic (cockroach).
