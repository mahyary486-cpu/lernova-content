# 关系代词 as 与 whose

## whose

表示**所属关系**，既可指人也可指物，后面接名词。

- The girl **whose father** is a doctor is my friend.
- The house **whose roof** is red is mine.（= the house the roof of which is red）

whose 在从句中作**定语**，不能被省略。

## as

**1. the same ... as / such ... as / as ... as**

- This is **the same** book **as** I lost yesterday.
- **Such** books **as** you mentioned are very useful.

**2. 引导非限制性定语从句**（指代整个主句，可放句首、句中、句尾）

- **As** we all know, the earth goes around the sun.
- He is a teacher, **as** is known to all.
- The sun rises in the east, **as** everyone knows.

## as 与 which 的区别

| | as | which |
|---|---|---|
| 位置 | 可在句首 | 不能在句首 |
| 含义 | 如同……（常有 as is known / as we know） | 这一点…… |

**例：** He passed the exam, **which** surprised everyone.（不能用 as）
