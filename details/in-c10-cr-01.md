# Chemical equations and balancing

A chemical equation shows reactants on the left and products on the right using symbols and formulae. A balanced equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides, in agreement with the law of conservation of mass.

Example: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Physical states (s), (l), (g), (aq) and conditions can be written in the equation.
