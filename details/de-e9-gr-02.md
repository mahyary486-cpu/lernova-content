# Simple Past, Past Continuous und Present Perfect

## Simple Past
Abgeschlossene Handlungen in der Vergangenheit: *They played football yesterday.* Regelmäßige Verben bekommen -ed, unregelmäßige haben eigene Formen (go, went, gone). Verneinung und Frage mit did: *He didn't go. Did you see her?*

## Past Continuous
Handlung im Verlauf, oft unterbrochen: *She was watching TV when the phone rang.* Bildung: was/were + -ing.

## Present Perfect
Verbindet Vergangenheit und Gegenwart: have/has + Partizip. *I have lived here for five years. Have you ever eaten sushi?* For + Dauer, since + Zeitpunkt, already, yet, ever, never.
Präsens Perfekt mit Dauer: *She has been playing the guitar for three years.*

## Past Perfect
Vorzeitigkeit: *When we arrived, the film had already started.* Bildung: had + Partizip.

Mit yesterday, last week oder ago steht immer das Simple Past.
