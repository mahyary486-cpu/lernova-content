# Friction and circular motion

Static friction fₛ ≤ μₛN, kinetic friction fₖ = μₖN, with μₖ < μₛ. The angle of repose equals the angle of friction (tan λ = μ). On an incline the force along the plane is mg sin θ and the normal force is mg cos θ.

The centripetal force is mv²/r; the safe speed on a level road is √(μrg).
