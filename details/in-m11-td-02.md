# Coordinates of a point

The coordinates (x, y, z) of a point are its signed distances from the YZ, ZX and XY planes. The octant is decided by the signs: (+, +, +) is octant I, (−, +, +) II, (−, −, +) III, (+, −, +) IV, (+, +, −) V, (−, +, −) VI, (−, −, −) VII and (+, −, −) VIII.

Example: the foot of the perpendicular from (1, 2, 3) to the XY-plane is (1, 2, 0).
