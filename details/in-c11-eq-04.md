# Buffers and solubility equilibria

A buffer (weak acid and its salt) resists pH change: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]). Ksp is the solubility product; for AgCl Ksp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] and s = √Ksp; precipitation occurs when the ionic product exceeds Ksp.

Salts hydrolyse: CH₃COONa is basic and NH₄Cl is acidic.
