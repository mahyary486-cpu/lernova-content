# Dalton's atomic theory, atoms and molecules

Dalton proposed that matter is made of atoms that cannot be created or destroyed in a chemical reaction; atoms of one element are identical, atoms of different elements differ, and atoms combine in fixed whole-number ratios.

An atom is the smallest particle of an element taking part in reactions. A molecule is a group of atoms bonded together, for example O₂ and H₂O. Elements have symbols such as Na, K and Fe.
