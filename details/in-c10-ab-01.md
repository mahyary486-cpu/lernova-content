# Acids and bases and their indicators

Acids taste sour, turn blue litmus red and produce H⁺ ions in water. Bases taste bitter, feel soapy, turn red litmus blue and the soluble ones (alkalis) produce OH⁻ ions in water.

Natural acids include citric acid (lemon), acetic acid (vinegar), lactic acid (curd), tartaric acid (tamarind) and methanoic acid (ant sting).
