# Matter Waves and the Bohr Atom

Atoms give off only certain colors of light because their electrons can only occupy certain energy levels.

## Key ideas
- **de Broglie**: **lambda = h / (m v)**.
- **Uncertainty**: delta x delta p is at least **h / (4 pi)**.
- **E_n = -13.6 eV / n^2** for hydrogen.
- Photon emitted: **h f = E_high - E_low**.
- Ground state energy **-13.6 eV**; ionization energy **13.6 eV**.
- Spectral lines are evidence of **discrete levels**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: de Broglie wavelength.** An electron moves at 1 x 10^6 m/s (m = 9.11 x 10^-31 kg).
1. lambda = h / (m v) = 6.63 x 10^-34 / (9.11 x 10^-31 x 10^6).
2. lambda = 7.3 x 10^-10 m.

**Example 2: Energy level.** Find E for n = 2 in hydrogen.
1. E_2 = -13.6 / 4.
2. E_2 = -3.4 eV.

**Example 3: Photon from a jump.** An electron falls from n = 3 to n = 2.
1. E_3 = -1.51 eV and E_2 = -3.40 eV.
2. Photon energy = 1.89 eV (red light, about 656 nm).

## Common mistakes
- Using the wrong sign for the energy levels.
- Thinking only light has a wavelength.
- Thinking the uncertainty principle is due to poor instruments.
