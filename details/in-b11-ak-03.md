# Non-chordates: Annelida to Hemichordata

Annelida (earthworm, metameric segmentation), Arthropoda (the largest phylum, jointed appendages, open circulation), Mollusca (mantle, radula, open circulation), Echinodermata (water vascular system, starfish) and Hemichordata (Balanoglossus).

Arthropods make up over two-thirds of animal species.
