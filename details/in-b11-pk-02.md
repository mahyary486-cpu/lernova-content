# Bryophytes and Pteridophytes

Bryophytes (liverworts such as Marchantia and mosses such as Funaria and Sphagnum) are non-vascular with a dominant gametophyte, and need water for fertilisation. Pteridophytes (ferns such as Dryopteris, Selaginella) are vascular cryptogams with a dominant sporophyte; the prothallus is the gametophyte.

Heterospory in Selaginella led to the seed habit.
