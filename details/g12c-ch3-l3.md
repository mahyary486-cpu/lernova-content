# Functional Groups and Their Compounds

The functional group tells you how a compound will behave, whatever the length of its carbon chain.

## Key ideas
- **-OH** alcohol; **-CHO** aldehyde; **C=O** ketone; **-COOH** carboxylic acid; **-COO-** ester; **-NH2** amine.
- **Primary alcohol -> aldehyde -> acid**; **secondary alcohol -> ketone**.
- Alcohols have high boiling points because of **hydrogen bonding**.
- **Esterification**: acid + alcohol <-> ester + water.
- Carboxylic acids are **weak acids**.
- Esters often have **fruity smells**.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Identify.** Name the functional group in CH3CH2CHO.
1. It ends in -CHO.
2. An aldehyde (propanal).

**Example 2: Oxidation.** What is formed when ethanol is fully oxidized?
1. Ethanol -> ethanal -> ethanoic acid.
2. Ethanoic acid.

**Example 3: Esterification.** What ester forms from methanol and ethanoic acid?
1. The alcohol part gives the first name and the acid gives the second.
2. Methyl ethanoate (and water).

## Common mistakes
- Confusing aldehydes and ketones.
- Forgetting the water produced in esterification.
- Thinking tertiary alcohols oxidize easily.
