# Molecular orbital theory and hydrogen bonding

Bond order = (bonding − antibonding electrons)/2: N₂ 3, O₂ 2, H₂ 1, He₂ 0. O₂ is paramagnetic (two unpaired electrons) and N₂ diamagnetic. A higher bond order means a shorter, stronger bond.

Hydrogen bonding in water explains the open structure and low density of ice.
