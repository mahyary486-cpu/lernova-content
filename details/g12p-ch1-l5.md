# Capacitors in Electric Fields

A camera flash stores energy slowly in a capacitor and releases it in a fraction of a second.

## Key ideas
- **C = Q / V**; unit **farad (F)**.
- Parallel plates: **C = epsilon_0 A / d**; with dielectric **kappa**: C = kappa epsilon_0 A / d.
- Energy: **U = 1/2 C V^2**.
- **Parallel**: C = C1 + C2; **series**: 1 / C = 1 / C1 + 1 / C2.
- A **dielectric** raises C.
- Doubling the plate separation **halves** C.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Capacitance.** A capacitor stores 6 x 10^-6 C at 12 V.
1. C = Q / V = 6 x 10^-6 / 12.
2. C = 5 x 10^-7 F = 0.5 microfarad.

**Example 2: Energy.** A 10 microfarad capacitor is charged to 100 V.
1. U = 1/2 C V^2 = 0.5 x 10 x 10^-6 x 10,000.
2. U = 0.05 J.

**Example 3: Series.** A 4 microfarad and a 12 microfarad capacitor in series.
1. 1 / C = 1 / 4 + 1 / 12 = 4 / 12.
2. C = 3 microfarads.

## Common mistakes
- Adding series capacitors directly.
- Forgetting the 1/2 in the energy formula.
- Using microfarads as farads.
