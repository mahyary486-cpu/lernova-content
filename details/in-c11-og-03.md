# Electronic displacements and reaction intermediates

Inductive effect (sigma bonds), resonance effect (pi electrons and lone pairs) and hyperconjugation. Homolytic fission gives free radicals and heterolytic fission gives ions: carbocations and carbanions. Carbocation stability: 3° > 2° > 1° > methyl.

Nucleophiles are electron-rich and electrophiles are electron-deficient.
