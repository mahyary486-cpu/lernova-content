# Graphs of Trigonometric Functions

## The basic graphs
- **y = sin x** starts at (0, 0), rises to 1 at π/2, returns to 0 at π, falls to -1 at 3π/2, and ends the cycle at 2π.
- **y = cos x** starts at its maximum (0, 1) and crosses zero at π/2.
- Both have domain all real numbers, range **-1 ≤ y ≤ 1** and **period 2π**.
- **y = tan x** has period **π** and vertical asymptotes at x = π/2 + nπ. Its range is all real numbers.

## The general form
**y = A sin(B(x - C)) + D** (and the same for cosine)
| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amplitude** | abs(A), the distance from the midline to a peak |
| **Period** | **2π / B** (for tangent: π / B) |
| **Phase shift** | C units to the right (left if C is negative) |
| **Midline** | y = D |

The **range** is D - abs(A) ≤ y ≤ D + abs(A).
A negative A reflects the graph over the midline.

## Examples
- y = 3 sin x: amplitude **3**, period 2π.
- y = sin 2x: period 2π/2 = **π**.
- y = cos(x/2): B = 1/2, period 2π/(1/2) = **4π**.
- y = 4 cos 3x: amplitude 4, period **2π/3**.
- y = 2 sin x + 1: midline **y = 1**, range -1 ≤ y ≤ 3.
- y = 2 sin x - 3: range **-5 ≤ y ≤ -1**.
- y = sin(x - π/2): the graph of sine shifted **right π/2**. This is the same graph as y = -cos x.
- y = 2 sin(x - π/2) + 1: amplitude 2, period 2π, shift right π/2, midline y = 1, maximum **3**, minimum -1.

## Reading a graph
- The midline is halfway between the maximum and minimum: (max + min)/2.
- The amplitude is (max - min)/2.
- The period is the horizontal length of one full cycle.

## Common mistakes
- Using B as the period. The period is 2π divided by B.
- Reading the shift with the wrong sign: x - C shifts right.
- Forgetting that the amplitude is never negative.
