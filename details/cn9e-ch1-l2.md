# 一般过去时与过去进行时

## 一般过去时

表示**过去发生的动作或状态**。

- 标志词：yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2020
- 规则动词加 **-ed**：worked, played；
- 不规则动词要记忆：go → **went**，have → **had**，see → **saw**，buy → **bought**，come → **came**。

**例句：** He **went** to Beijing last week. I **bought** a new bike yesterday.

## 过去进行时

表示**过去某一时刻（或某段时间）正在进行**的动作。结构：**was / were + 动词 -ing**。

**例句：**

- I **was reading** when you called me.
- What **were** you **doing** at 8 o'clock last night?

## when 和 while

- **while** + 进行时（持续动作）：While I was cooking, my mother came in.
- **when** 可引导短暂动作：I was reading when the phone rang.
