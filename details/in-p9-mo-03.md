# Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity: a = (v − u) / t, in m/s². Positive acceleration means speeding up; negative acceleration (retardation) means slowing down.

Example: from 0 to 20 m/s in 4 s gives a = 5 m/s². In a velocity–time graph the slope is the acceleration and the area under the graph is the distance. In uniform circular motion the speed is constant, yet the velocity changes because its direction changes.
