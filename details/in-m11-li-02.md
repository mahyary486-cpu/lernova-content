# Rules for solving and the number line

Adding or subtracting the same number keeps the sign. Multiplying or dividing by a positive number keeps the sign, by a negative number reverses it. Solutions are shown on the number line with open circles for strict inequalities and filled circles for ≤ and ≥.

Absolute-value inequalities: ∣x∣ < a gives −a < x < a and ∣x∣ > a gives x < −a or x > a.
