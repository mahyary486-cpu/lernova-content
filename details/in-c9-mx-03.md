# Concentration of solutions

Mass percentage = (mass of solute / mass of solution) × 100. If 10 g of salt is dissolved in 90 g of water, the solution weighs 100 g and the concentration is 10%.

Mass by volume percentage = (mass of solute / volume of solution in mL) × 100. A saturated solution cannot dissolve more solute at that temperature; the solubility of most solids increases with temperature.
