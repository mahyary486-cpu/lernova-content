# Segments, Angles and Measurement

## Segments
- The **length** of segment AB is written AB. It is a number.
- Segments with equal lengths are **congruent**.
- The **midpoint** of a segment splits it into two congruent segments.
- **Segment Addition Postulate:** if B is between A and C, then AB + BC = AC.

## Angles
An angle is made of two rays (the sides) that share an endpoint (the vertex). It is measured in degrees.

| Type | Measure |
|---|---|
| Acute | less than 90 degrees |
| Right | exactly 90 degrees |
| Obtuse | more than 90 and less than 180 degrees |
| Straight | exactly 180 degrees |

- An **angle bisector** is a ray that splits an angle into two equal angles.
- **Angle Addition Postulate:** if ray OB is inside angle AOC, then angle AOB + angle BOC = angle AOC.

## Special pairs of angles
- **Complementary angles** add up to 90 degrees.
- **Supplementary angles** add up to 180 degrees.
- **Adjacent angles** share a vertex and a side and do not overlap.
- A **linear pair** is two adjacent angles whose outer sides form a line, so they are supplementary.
- **Vertical angles** are opposite angles formed by two crossing lines. They are always equal.

## Worked examples
1. B is between A and C. AB = 2x + 1, BC = 3x - 4 and AC = 22. Then 2x + 1 + 3x - 4 = 22, so 5x - 3 = 22 and x = 5. Then AB = 11 and BC = 11.
2. M is the midpoint of PQ and PM = 9. Then MQ = 9 and PQ = 18.
3. Ray BD bisects angle ABC and angle ABC = 100 degrees. Each part is 50 degrees.
4. The complement of a 35 degree angle is 90 - 35 = 55 degrees. The supplement is 180 - 35 = 145 degrees.
5. Two lines cross. One angle is 72 degrees. The vertical angle is also 72 degrees. The other two angles are each 180 - 72 = 108 degrees.

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting that the three points must be in order for the Segment Addition Postulate: B has to be between A and C.
- Mixing up complementary (90) and supplementary (180).
- Thinking vertical angles are supplementary. They are equal.
