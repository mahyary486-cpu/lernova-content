# Applications of counting

Counting problems combine permutations and combinations: committees with conditions, arrangements with objects together, diagonals of polygons (ⁿC₂ − n), triangles from points and handshakes (ⁿC₂).

Example: a committee of 4 from 6 men and 4 women with at least one woman: ¹⁰C₄ − ⁶C₄ = 195.
