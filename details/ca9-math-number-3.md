# Ratios, rates and proportions

A **ratio** compares quantities of the same kind (3 : 5). A **rate** compares quantities with different units. A **unit rate** has 1 in the denominator: $12 for 4 L is $3 per litre.

**Proportions.** Two ratios are equal: 3 / 5 = x / 20. Cross-multiply: 5x = 60, so x = 12.

**Scale.** If a map scale is 1 cm : 5 km, then 3.5 cm represents 3.5 × 5 = 17.5 km.

**Speed.** speed = distance ÷ time. Travelling 150 km in 2.5 h gives 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 km/h.
