# Indirekte Rede (Reported Speech)

## Zeitverschiebung
Die Zeit rutscht um eine Stufe zurück: Simple Present → Simple Past, will → would, Present Perfect → Past Perfect. *'I am tired,' she said. → She said that she was tired.*

## Anpassung
Pronomen und Zeitangaben ändern sich: tomorrow → the next day, today → that day.

## Fragen
Aussagesatzstellung, kein do: *'Where do you live?' → He asked me where I lived.* Ja/Nein-Fragen mit whether oder if.

## Befehle
*'Close the door!' → He told me to close the door.* Verneint: *not to touch.*
