# Kepler's laws and the law of gravitation

Kepler's laws: planets move in ellipses with the Sun at a focus; the line joining a planet to the Sun sweeps equal areas in equal times; T² ∝ a³. Newton's law of gravitation: F = G m₁m₂ / r² with G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻².

Gravity is always attractive and varies as 1/r².
