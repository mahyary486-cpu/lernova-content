# Decimal expansions of rational numbers

A rational number has a terminating or non-terminating repeating decimal expansion; an irrational number has a non-terminating non-repeating one.

If p/q is in lowest terms, the decimal terminates exactly when the prime factorisation of q is of the form 2ⁿ5ᵐ. So 7/8 terminates but 1/7 repeats.
