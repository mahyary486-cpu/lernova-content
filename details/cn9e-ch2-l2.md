# for 和 since 与延续性动词

## 表示一段时间

- **for** + 一段时间：for two years, for a long time；
- **since** + 过去的时间点 / 从句：since 2020, since I was a child, since he came here。

**例句：**

- He has lived here **for** ten years.
- He has lived here **since** 2015.

## 延续性动词与非延续性动词

与 for、since 连用的动词必须是**延续性动词**（动作可以持续）：be, live, stay, know, have, keep。

**非延续性动词**（瞬间完成）在肯定句中不能与 for / since 连用，要改成延续性动词：

| 非延续性动词 | 延续性表达 |
|---|---|
| buy | have |
| join | be in / be a member of |
| die | be dead |
| come / arrive | be here / be there |
| leave | be away |
| open | be open |

**例句：**

- 错：He has bought the bike for two years.
- 对：He **has had** the bike for two years.
- 对：She **has been in** this school for two years.
