# Relative pronouns: who, which, that, whose e where

## Pronomes

- **who:** pessoas. *A teacher is a person who teaches.*
- **which:** coisas. *The car which is red is mine.*
- **that:** pessoas ou coisas (orações restritivas). *The book that I bought is great.*
- **whose:** posse. *The girl whose bike was stolen is crying.*
- **where:** lugar. *This is the place where I was born.*
- **when:** tempo. *The day when we met was sunny.*
- **why:** motivo. *The reason why he left is unknown.*

## Omissão do pronome

Quando o pronome é **objeto** da oração relativa, ele pode ser omitido:

- *The film (that) we watched was long.*
- *The woman (who) I met yesterday is my aunt.*

Quando é **sujeito**, não pode ser omitido: *The man who lives here is kind.*

## Observações

- **everything, all, nothing** costumam pedir **that**: *Everything that he said was true.*
- Com preposição, em estilo formal: *The person to whom I spoke is the manager.*
- Não confunda **whose** (posse) com **who's** (= who is): *Whose umbrella is this?*
