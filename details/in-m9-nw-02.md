# Irrational numbers and the number line

The square root of a non-perfect square is irrational: √2, √3, √5. The number √4 = 2 is rational. √2 can be placed on the number line by constructing the diagonal of a unit square.

Between any two real numbers there are infinitely many rational and infinitely many irrational numbers. Sums and products: √2 + 1 and √2 + √3 are irrational, but √2 − √2 = 0 and (√2)² = 2 are rational. The product of a non-zero rational and an irrational is irrational.
