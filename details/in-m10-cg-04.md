# Applications of the section formula

The centroid of a triangle with vertices (x₁, y₁), (x₂, y₂), (x₃, y₃) is ((x₁ + x₂ + x₃)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + y₃)/3). To find where an axis divides a segment, put the relevant coordinate equal to 0.

Example: the x-axis divides the segment joining (2, −3) and (5, 6) in the ratio 1 : 2.
