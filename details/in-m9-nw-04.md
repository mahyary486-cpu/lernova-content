# Exponents and surds

Laws of exponents: aᵐ · aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ, aᵐ / aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ, (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ, (ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ, a⁰ = 1, a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ. Fractional powers are roots: 16^(1/2) = 4 and 27^(1/3) = 3.

For square roots: √a · √b = √(ab), and (√a)² = a. To rationalise 1/√2 multiply by √2/√2 to get √2/2. For 1/(√3 + √2) multiply by (√3 − √2)/(√3 − √2) to get √3 − √2.
