# Irrational numbers

A number is irrational if it cannot be written as p/q with integers p, q and q ≠ 0. Examples are √2, √3, √5 and π. √2 is proved irrational by contradiction: assume √2 = p/q in lowest terms and show p and q share the factor 2.

The sum or difference of a rational and an irrational is irrational, and so is the product or quotient of a non-zero rational and an irrational, for example 3 + √5 and 2√3.
