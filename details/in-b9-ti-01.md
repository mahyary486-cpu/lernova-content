# Plant tissues: meristematic and permanent

Meristematic tissue has actively dividing cells. Apical meristem at the tips increases length, and lateral meristem increases girth. Permanent tissues form from meristem cells that have stopped dividing and have a fixed function.

Simple permanent tissues are parenchyma (storage, chlorenchyma for photosynthesis), collenchyma (flexible support) and sclerenchyma (hard, lignified support).
