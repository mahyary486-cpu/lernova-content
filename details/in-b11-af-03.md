# Anatomy of root, stem and leaf

The dicot root has 2–4 xylem bundles and the monocot root is polyarch; the dicot stem has open bundles in a ring and the monocot stem closed scattered bundles. A dicot leaf is dorsiventral (palisade parenchyma) and a monocot leaf isobilateral (bulliform cells).

The endodermis has Casparian strips.
