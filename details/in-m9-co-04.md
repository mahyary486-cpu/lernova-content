# Reflection, distance and midpoint

Reflection in the x-axis changes the sign of y: (3, 4) becomes (3, −4). Reflection in the y-axis changes the sign of x: (3, 4) becomes (−3, 4). Reflection in the origin changes both signs.

Along a horizontal or vertical line, the length of a segment is the difference of the coordinates: from (2, 3) to (2, 8) the length is 5. The distance from the origin to (3, 4) is 5 by the Pythagoras theorem.

The midpoint of the segment joining (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) is ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2). Area of a rectangle or triangle on the plane follows from the lengths of its sides.
