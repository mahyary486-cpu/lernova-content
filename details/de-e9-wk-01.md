# Adjektive und Adverbien steigern

## Steigerung
Kurze Adjektive: -er / -est (*big, bigger, the biggest; happy, happier, the happiest*). Lange Adjektive: more / most (*more beautiful, the most beautiful*). Unregelmäßig: good, better, best; bad, worse, worst.

## Vergleiche
Ungleichheit: *She is taller than her brother.* Gleichheit: *He is as tall as his father.*

## Adverbien
Adverb = Adjektiv + -ly (*quick, quickly*). Ausnahmen: good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard. Auch Adverbien lassen sich steigern: *She speaks more fluently.*

## Je ... desto
*The more you practise, the better you get.*
