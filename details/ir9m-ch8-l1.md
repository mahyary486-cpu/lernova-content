# Surface area

Surface area is the total area of all the outside faces of a solid.

## Key ideas

- Surface area is measured in square units, such as cm^2 or m^2.
- A net is the flat shape you get when you unfold a solid. Adding the areas of the parts of the net gives the surface area.
- Cuboid with length l, width w and height h: S = 2(lw + lh + wh). A cube with edge a has S = 6a^2.
- Prism: S = 2 × (base area) + (base perimeter) × height. The second part is called the lateral area.
- Cylinder with radius r and height h: the lateral part unfolds into a rectangle of width 2πr and height h. So S = 2πr^2 + 2πrh.
- Cone with radius r and slant height s: S = πr^2 + πrs. The slant height is found with s^2 = r^2 + h^2.
- Sphere with radius r: S = 4πr^2.
- Use π = 3.14 unless the question says otherwise, or leave the answer in terms of π.

## Worked examples

Example 1. Find the surface area of a cuboid with l = 5 cm, w = 3 cm, h = 4 cm.
Step 1: lw = 5 × 3 = 15, lh = 5 × 4 = 20, wh = 3 × 4 = 12.
Step 2: Add them: 15 + 20 + 12 = 47.
Step 3: Multiply by 2: S = 2 × 47 = 94 cm^2.

Example 2. A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Find its surface area. Use π = 3.14.
Step 1: Area of the two bases = 2 × π × 3^2 = 18π.
Step 2: Lateral area = 2 × π × 3 × 10 = 60π.
Step 3: Total = 18π + 60π = 78π.
Step 4: With π = 3.14, S = 78 × 3.14 = 244.92 cm^2.

Example 3. A cone has radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find its total surface area. Use π = 3.14.
Step 1: Find the slant height: s^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100, so s = 10 cm.
Step 2: Base area = π × 6^2 = 36π.
Step 3: Lateral area = π × 6 × 10 = 60π.
Step 4: Total = 36π + 60π = 96π.
Step 5: With π = 3.14, S = 96 × 3.14 = 301.44 cm^2.

## Common mistakes

- Using the height h instead of the slant height s in the cone formula for the lateral area.
- Forgetting one of the two bases of a cylinder or prism.
- Mixing units, for example centimeters and meters, in one calculation.
- Writing cubic units (cm^3) for an area. Surface area always uses square units.

## Remember

- Surface area is the sum of the areas of all faces: bases plus lateral area.
- Draw the net if you are not sure which parts to add.
- The sphere formula S = 4πr^2 has no height, only the radius.
