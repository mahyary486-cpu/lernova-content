# Degrees of freedom, equipartition and mean free path

Each quadratic term contributes ½kT per molecule (equipartition). A monatomic molecule has 3 degrees of freedom and a rigid diatomic molecule 5, so U = (3/2)RT and (5/2)RT per mole, and γ = 1 + 2/f.

The mean free path λ = 1/(√2 π n d²) decreases when the pressure increases.
