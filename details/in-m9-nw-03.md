# Decimal expansions

A rational number has a decimal expansion that is either terminating (0.25) or non-terminating and recurring (1/3 = 0.333...). An irrational number has a non-terminating and non-recurring expansion (√2 = 1.41421...).

To convert a recurring decimal to a fraction, let x be the number and multiply by 10 or 100 to line up the repeating part, then subtract. Example: x = 0.3636... gives 99x = 36, so x = 4/11. Also 0.666... = 2/3 and 0.999... = 1.
