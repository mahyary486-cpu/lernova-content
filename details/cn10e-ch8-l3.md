# 应用文写作

## 书信格式

> Dear Tom,
>
> （开头）I'm writing to ...
>
> （正文）...
>
> （结尾）I'm looking forward to your reply.
>
> Yours,
> Li Hua

- **称呼**写在信的开头，顶格；
- **署名**写在信的结尾，右下方。

## 常用句型

**邀请信：**

- I'm writing to **invite you to** our party.
- It would be a great pleasure if you could come.

**建议信：**

- Here are some suggestions for you.
- In my opinion, you should ...

**申请信：**

- I'm writing to **apply for** the position of ...
- I'm sure that I'm qualified for the job.

**结尾：**

- I'm looking forward to your reply.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.

## 常用过渡词

First, Then, Besides, What's more, However, In a word

## 写作步骤

1. **审题**：文体、人称、时态、要点；
2. **列提纲**；
3. **写作**：要点齐全，句型多样；
4. **检查**：拼写、语法、标点。
