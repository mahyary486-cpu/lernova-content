# Types of redox reactions and balancing

Types: combination, decomposition, displacement and disproportionation (the same element is oxidised and reduced, as in 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂). Balancing by the oxidation number method uses the equality of total increase and decrease.

Cl₂ + 2OH⁻ → Cl⁻ + ClO⁻ + H₂O is a disproportionation.
