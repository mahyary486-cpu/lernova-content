# Gymnosperms and Angiosperms

Gymnosperms (Pinus, Cycas) have naked seeds, cones with micro- and megasporophylls and mycorrhizal or coralloid roots. Angiosperms have flowers and fruits, are monocots (one cotyledon) or dicots (two cotyledons) and show double fertilisation with triploid endosperm.

The typical embryo sac is 7-celled and 8-nucleate.
