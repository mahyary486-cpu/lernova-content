# Solutions, suspensions and colloids

In a solution, the solute dissolves in the solvent; its particles are tiny and it does not scatter light. A suspension has large particles that settle on standing. A colloid (milk, fog) has particles of intermediate size that do not settle but scatter light (Tyndall effect) and show Brownian motion.

Alloys such as brass are homogeneous mixtures of metals. Fog is liquid droplets in a gas.
