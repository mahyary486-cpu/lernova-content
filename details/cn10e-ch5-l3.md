# 含蓄条件与 suggest 等引导的从句

## 含蓄条件句

条件不用 if 引导，而是隐含在**介词短语**或**上下文**中：

- **without / but for**：**Without** your help, I **couldn't have succeeded**.
- **otherwise / or**：I was busy; **otherwise** I **would have gone** with you.
- **with**：**With** more money, he **could buy** a bigger house.

## suggest 等动词后的宾语从句

**suggest, advise, demand, order, insist, require, request, propose + that 从句：**

从句谓语用 **(should) + 动词原形**，**should 可以省略**。

- The doctor **suggested** that he **(should) stay** in bed.
- They **demanded** that we **(should) leave** at once.

## 形容词后的主语从句

**important, necessary, essential, natural, strange + that 从句：**

- It is **necessary** that he **(should) come** on time.
- It is **strange** that he **should say** so.

## 注意（不虚拟的情况）

- **suggest** 表示"**暗示、表明**"，**insist** 表示"**坚持认为**（事实）"时，用**陈述语气**：
  - His look **suggested** that he **was** angry.
  - He **insisted** that he **was** right.
