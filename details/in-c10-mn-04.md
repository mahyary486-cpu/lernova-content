# Extraction, refining and corrosion

Ores are minerals from which a metal can be profitably extracted. Reactive metals (Na, Al) are obtained by electrolytic reduction, metals of middle reactivity (Zn, Fe) by reduction of oxides with carbon after roasting or calcination, and mercury by heating cinnabar in air.

Electrolytic refining deposits pure copper on the cathode. Alloys like brass, bronze and solder are made to improve properties. Galvanisation prevents rusting.
