# Fundamental principle of counting

Multiplication principle: if one event can occur in m ways and, following it, another in n ways, the two events together can occur in m × n ways. Addition principle: if two exclusive events can occur in m and n ways, one or the other can occur in m + n ways.

Example: there are 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 three-digit numbers from the digits 1 to 5 without repetition.
