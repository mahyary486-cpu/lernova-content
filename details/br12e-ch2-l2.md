# Linking words: contraste, motivo e resultado

## Contraste

- **Although / even though / though** + **oração**: *Although it was raining, we went out.*
- **Despite / in spite of** + **substantivo ou -ing**: *Despite being tired, he finished the work.*
- **However** liga duas frases: *She is rich; however, she is not happy.*
- **Though** pode ficar no fim da frase: *It's cheap, though.*

## Motivo

- **Because** + oração: *She was late because there was traffic.*
- **Because of** + substantivo: *She was late because of the traffic.*

## Resultado

- **So** + oração: *It was late, so we went home.*
- **Therefore / as a result** + oração: *He studied hard; therefore, he passed.*

## Outras construções

- *Not only is she smart, but she is also kind.* (inversão após *not only*)
- *Despite the fact that she was tired, she kept working.* (com oração)

## Resumo

| Ideia | Palavras |
|---|---|
| contraste | although, even though, though, despite, in spite of, however |
| motivo | because, because of |
| resultado | so, therefore, as a result |
