# 让步与目的状语从句

## 让步状语从句

引导词：**although / though**（虽然），**even if / even though**（即使）

- **Although** it was raining, they went out.
- **Though** he is old, he works hard.

**注意：although / though 和 but 不能连用**，但可以和 yet、still 连用。

- ❌ Although he is poor, but he is happy.
- ✅ Although he is poor, he is happy.
- ✅ He is poor, but he is happy.

## 目的状语从句

引导词：**so that, in order that**（以便）

- He studied hard **so that** he could pass the exam.
- Speak louder **so that** everyone can hear you.

## 目的的其他表达

用不定式 **in order to / so as to**（不带从句）：

- He studied hard **in order to** pass the exam.
- We got up early **so as to** catch the first bus.

## 区别

**so that**（目的）和 **so ... that**（结果）不同：

- He ran so fast that he caught the bus.（结果）
- He ran fast so that he could catch the bus.（目的）
