# 原因与结果状语从句

## 原因状语从句

引导词：**because, since, as**

- **because**：语气最强，回答 why 的问题：
  - — Why were you late? — **Because** the bus broke down.
- **since**：既然（已知的原因）：**Since** you are here, let's start.
- **as**：由于，语气较弱，常放在句首。

**注意：because 和 so 不能连用。**

- ❌ Because it rained, so we stayed at home.
- ✅ Because it rained, we stayed at home.
- ✅ It rained, so we stayed at home.

## 结果状语从句：so ... that 和 such ... that

意思是"如此……以至于……"。

- **so + 形容词 / 副词 + that**：
  - He was **so tired that** he fell asleep at once.
  - She runs **so fast that** nobody can catch her.
- **such + (a / an) + (形容词) + 名词 + that**：
  - It was **such a nice day that** we went out.
  - They are **such good friends that** they never fight.

## 选择 so 还是 such

看后面是**名词**还是**形容词 / 副词**：有名词用 such，没有名词用 so。
