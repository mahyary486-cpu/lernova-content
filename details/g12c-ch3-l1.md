# Hydrocarbons: Alkanes and Their Names

Natural gas, petrol and candle wax are all alkanes of different chain lengths.

## Key ideas
- **Alkanes**: **CnH2n+2**, only **single** bonds.
- Prefixes: **meth-, eth-, prop-, but-, pent-, hex-, hept-, oct-**.
- Name = **branches + longest chain**; number to give the **lowest** numbers.
- **Cycloalkanes**: **CnH2n**.
- Boiling point **increases** with chain length.
- Reactions: **combustion** and **substitution** with halogens.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Formula.** Give the formula of the alkane with 10 carbons.
1. CnH2n+2 with n = 10.
2. C10H22 (decane).

**Example 2: Naming.** Name CH3-CH(CH3)-CH2-CH3.
1. The longest chain has 4 carbons: butane, with a methyl on carbon 2.
2. 2-methylbutane.

**Example 3: Combustion.** Write the balanced equation for burning propane.
1. C3H8 + O2 -> CO2 + H2O: 3 CO2 and 4 H2O.
2. C3H8 + 5 O2 -> 3 CO2 + 4 H2O.

## Common mistakes
- Counting the wrong longest chain.
- Numbering from the wrong end.
- Forgetting the 2n + 2 rule.
