# Ethanol, ethanoic acid, soaps and detergents

Ethanol reacts with sodium to give hydrogen, and on heating with concentrated H₂SO₄ at 443 K gives ethene. Ethanoic acid reacts with sodium hydrogencarbonate to give CO₂. An alcohol and a carboxylic acid form an ester.

Soaps are sodium or potassium salts of fatty acids made by saponification. They form micelles that trap dirt but form scum in hard water; detergents do not.
