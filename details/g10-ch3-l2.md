# Congruent Triangles: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS

## Congruent figures
Two triangles are **congruent** if they have exactly the same shape and size. Then all three pairs of corresponding sides and all three pairs of corresponding angles are congruent. We write triangle ABC is congruent to triangle DEF, and the order of the letters shows which parts match.

## Ways to prove triangles congruent
| Shortcut | What you need |
|---|---|
| **SSS** | all three pairs of sides congruent |
| **SAS** | two pairs of sides and the **included** angle (the angle between them) congruent |
| **ASA** | two pairs of angles and the **included** side congruent |
| **AAS** | two pairs of angles and a **non-included** side congruent |
| **HL** | right triangles: the hypotenuse and one leg congruent |

**These do not work:**
- **SSA** (two sides and an angle that is not between them), because the triangle may not be determined.
- **AAA** (three angles), because it only gives similar triangles, not congruent ones.

## CPCTC
**C**orresponding **P**arts of **C**ongruent **T**riangles are **C**ongruent. First prove the triangles congruent, then use CPCTC to say that other pairs of sides or angles are equal.

## Worked examples
1. Triangle ABC has AB = 5, BC = 7, AC = 9. Triangle DEF has DE = 5, EF = 7, DF = 9. They are congruent by **SSS**.
2. In triangles ABC and DEF, AB = DE = 6, AC = DF = 8 and angle A = angle D = 50 degrees. The angle is between the two sides, so they are congruent by **SAS**.
3. Two triangles have angles of 40 and 70 degrees and the side between those angles equal. This is **ASA**.
4. Two triangles have angles of 40 and 70 degrees and a side that is not between them equal. This is **AAS**.
5. Two right triangles each have a hypotenuse of 13 and a leg of 5. They are congruent by **HL**, so the other legs are both 12.
6. Use CPCTC: M is the midpoint of both AB and CD (the segments cross at M). Triangles AMC and BMD have AM = BM, vertical angles at M equal and CM = DM, so they are congruent by SAS. By CPCTC, AC = BD.

## Common mistakes
- Using SSA. Two sides and a non-included angle are not enough.
- Using AAA. Equal angles do not make triangles the same size.
- Using CPCTC **before** proving the triangles congruent.
- Matching the letters in the wrong order when you write the congruence statement.
