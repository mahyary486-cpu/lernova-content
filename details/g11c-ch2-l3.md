# The Ideal Gas Law

One equation can describe the air in a room, a balloon or a scuba tank.

## Key ideas
- **PV = nRT**.
- **R = 0.0821 L atm/(mol K)** or **8.31 J/(mol K)**.
- T in **kelvins**; match the units of R.
- **Density**: d = P M / (R T).
- **Molar mass**: M = m R T / (P V).
- **22.4 L/mol** at STP.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Moles.** How many moles of gas are in 5.0 L at 2.0 atm and 300 K (R = 0.0821)?
1. n = PV / (RT) = 2.0 x 5.0 / (0.0821 x 300).
2. n = 10 / 24.6 = 0.41 mol.

**Example 2: Pressure.** 2.0 mol of gas occupies 10 L at 300 K.
1. P = nRT / V = 2.0 x 0.0821 x 300 / 10.
2. P = 4.9 atm.

**Example 3: Molar mass.** 1.6 g of gas occupies 1.0 L at 1.0 atm and 273 K (R = 0.0821).
1. n = PV / (RT) = 1.0 / (0.0821 x 273) = 0.0446 mol.
2. M = 1.6 / 0.0446 = 35.9 g/mol.

## Common mistakes
- Using the wrong value of R for the units.
- Forgetting to convert Celsius to kelvins.
- Using volume in milliliters.
