# Density and Pressure

A person standing on one foot presses harder on the ground than on two feet, because the same weight acts on a smaller area.

## Key ideas
- **Density** rho = m / V (kg/m^3). Water = **1000 kg/m^3**.
- **Pressure** P = F / A. Unit: **pascal (Pa)** = 1 N/m^2.
- Same force, **smaller area** = **greater pressure**.
- **Atmospheric pressure** at sea level is about **1.01 x 10^5 Pa**.
- Atmospheric pressure **decreases with altitude**.
- **Absolute pressure** = gauge pressure + atmospheric pressure.

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Pressure.** A 200 N force acts on 0.5 m^2.
1. P = F / A = 200 / 0.5.
2. P = 400 Pa.

**Example 2: Pressure of feet.** A 60 kg person stands on two feet with total area 0.04 m^2 (g = 9.8).
1. F = 60 x 9.8 = 588 N.
2. P = 588 / 0.04 = 14,700 Pa.

**Example 3: Density.** A block has a mass of 600 kg and a volume of 0.2 m^3.
1. rho = m / V = 600 / 0.2.
2. rho = 3000 kg/m^3.

## Common mistakes
- Using the weight in kg instead of newtons in P = F / A.
- Confusing pressure with force.
- Forgetting to convert cm^2 to m^2.
