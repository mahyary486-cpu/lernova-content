# Word problems and reducible equations

To solve a word problem, choose variables, write two equations, solve them and check in the story. Typical problems involve ages, digits, fractions, fares and boats in streams.

Some equations reduce to linear form by substitution: for 2/x + 3/y = 13 and 5/x − 4/y = −2 put u = 1/x and v = 1/y, then solve for u and v.
