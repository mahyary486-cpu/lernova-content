# Pressure and Pascal's law

Pressure P = F/A in pascals; at depth h in a liquid P = P₀ + ρgh, where P₀ ≈ 1.01 × 10⁵ Pa is atmospheric pressure. Pascal's law: pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished in all directions, as in a hydraulic lift.

Archimedes' principle: the buoyant force equals the weight of the fluid displaced.
