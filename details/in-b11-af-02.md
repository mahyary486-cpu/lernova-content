# Tissue systems

The epidermal tissue system (epidermis, stomata with guard cells), the ground tissue system (cortex, pericycle, pith) and the vascular tissue system (xylem and phloem). Vascular bundles may be radial (roots), conjoint open (dicot stem) or conjoint closed (monocot stem).

Stomata are bean-shaped in dicots and dumb-bell shaped in grasses.
