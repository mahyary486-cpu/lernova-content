# Comparatives and superlatives

## Adjetivos curtos

- Comparativo: **-er + than** (tall → taller than);
- Superlativo: **the -est** (tall → the tallest).

Atenção à grafia: big → bigger → **the biggest**; hot → hotter; happy → happier → **the happiest**.

## Adjetivos longos

- Comparativo: **more + adjetivo + than** (more expensive than);
- Superlativo: **the most + adjetivo** (the most intelligent).

## Irregulares

- good → **better** → **the best**;
- bad → **worse** → **the worst**;
- far → farther/further → the farthest/furthest.

## Igualdade

**as + adjetivo + as**: *He is as tall as his father.* Negativa: *She is not as tall as her brother.*

## The more... the more

*The more you study, the more you learn.*

## Exemplos

- Russia is the **largest** country in the world.
- This is the **worst** movie I have ever seen.
- Football is much **more** popular than volleyball in Brazil.
