# 介词 + 关系代词

## 构成

**介词 + which**（指物），**介词 + whom**（指人）。

- This is the room **in which** he lives.（live **in** the room）
- The man **with whom** I talked is our teacher.（talk **with** the man）
- The reason **for which** he was late is unknown.

## 介词的选择

1. 看**先行词**与从句动词的搭配：
   - the pen **with which** he writes（write **with** a pen）
   - the school **at which** he studies
2. 看从句中的**动词**的固定搭配：
   - depend **on**, look **after**, listen **to**；
   - The man **to whom** I spoke is a doctor.

## 数词 / 不定代词 + of + which / whom

- He has three sons, **all of whom** are doctors.
- There are 50 students in the class, **most of whom** are girls.
- She has many books, **some of which** are in English.

## 简化

**介词 + which** 常可以转换成**关系副词**：

- the room **in which** he lives = the room **where** he lives；
- the day **on which** we met = the day **when** we met。

**注意**：**介词后不能用 that 和 who**。
