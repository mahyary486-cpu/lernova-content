# Fermentation and the Krebs cycle

Without oxygen, pyruvic acid is converted to ethanol and CO₂ in yeast or lactic acid in muscles. In aerobic respiration pyruvate forms acetyl CoA in the mitochondria and enters the TCA cycle, which gives per turn 3 NADH, 1 FADH₂, 1 GTP and 2 CO₂.

Hans Krebs discovered the cycle.
