# Projectile motion

A projectile launched at speed u at angle θ moves with constant horizontal velocity u cos θ and vertical acceleration g downwards. Time of flight T = 2u sin θ / g, maximum height H = u² sin²θ / 2g and range R = u² sin 2θ / g.

Range is maximum (u²/g) at 45°; angles θ and 90° − θ give the same range.
