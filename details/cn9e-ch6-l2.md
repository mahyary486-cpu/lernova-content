# must、have to、should

## must 与 have to

- **must**：必须，表示**主观**的需要；
  - You **must** finish your homework.
- **have to**：不得不，表示**客观**的需要，有人称和时态变化（has to, had to）；
  - I **have to** get up early every day because school starts at seven.

## 否定的区别

| 否定形式 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **mustn't** | **禁止**，不允许 | You mustn't smoke here. |
| **needn't / don't have to** | **不必**，没有必要 | You needn't hurry. |

## 回答 Must I ...? 的问句

- 肯定回答：Yes, you **must**.
- 否定回答：No, you **needn't**（或 don't have to），**不能**用 mustn't。

> — Must I finish it today? — No, you **needn't**.

## should / ought to：应该

- You **should** drink more water.
- 表示劝告、建议或义务，语气比 must 弱。
