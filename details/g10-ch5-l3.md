# Sine, Cosine and Tangent

## The three ratios
For an acute angle A in a right triangle:
- **Opposite** = the side across from angle A.
- **Adjacent** = the leg next to angle A (not the hypotenuse).
- **Hypotenuse** = the side across from the right angle.

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| sin A | opposite / hypotenuse |
| cos A | adjacent / hypotenuse |
| tan A | opposite / adjacent |

Memory aid: **SOH - CAH - TOA**.

## Useful facts
- The ratios depend only on the **angle**, not on the size of the triangle.
- sin A = cos (90 - A). The sine of an angle equals the cosine of its complement.
- Make sure your calculator is in **degree** mode.

## Common values
| Angle | sin | cos | tan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 degrees | 1/2 | (square root of 3)/2 | (square root of 3)/3 |
| 45 degrees | (square root of 2)/2 | (square root of 2)/2 | 1 |
| 60 degrees | (square root of 3)/2 | 1/2 | square root of 3 |

## Worked examples
1. In a 3-4-5 triangle, for the angle opposite the side 3: sin = 3/5, cos = 4/5, tan = 3/4.
2. A right triangle has hypotenuse 20 and angle 30 degrees. The side opposite it is 20 x sin 30 = 20 x 0.5 = **10**.
3. A ladder makes a 60 degree angle with the ground and is 12 feet long. It reaches 12 x sin 60, about **10.4 feet** up the wall.
4. A tree casts a shadow of 30 feet when the sun is 40 degrees above the horizon. The height is 30 x tan 40, about 30 x 0.839 = **25.2 feet**.
5. cos 20 = sin 70 because 20 and 70 are complementary.

## Common mistakes
- Using the wrong side as "opposite" or "adjacent". These words depend on the chosen angle.
- Forgetting that sine and cosine of an acute angle are always less than 1.
- Having the calculator in radian mode.
