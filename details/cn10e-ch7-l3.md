# 情态动词的完成形式

**情态动词 + have + 过去分词**，表示对**过去**的推测，或对过去的**遗憾、批评**。

## 对过去的推测

| 结构 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **must have done** | 一定已经…… | He must have left already. |
| **can't / couldn't have done** | 不可能已经…… | He can't have left. |
| **may / might have done** | 可能已经…… | He may have missed the bus. |

## 虚拟意义（过去没有做成）

| 结构 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **should have done** | 本应该做而没做 | You **should have told** me earlier. |
| **ought to have done** | 本应该做而没做 | You ought to have helped her. |
| **needn't have done** | 本没必要做却做了 | You **needn't have bought** it. |
| **could have done** | 本来能够做而没做 | I **could have won** the race. |
| **would have done** | 本来会做 | I would have come, but I was ill. |

## 区别

- **should have done**：做了不好的事 or 没做该做的事（遗憾 / 批评）；
- **needn't have done**：做了没必要的事。

## 例题

— The light is still on. — He **must have forgotten** to turn it off.
