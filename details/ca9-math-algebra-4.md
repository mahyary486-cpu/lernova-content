# Equations of lines and linear models

**Slope–intercept form:** y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept. For y = 3x − 5, the slope is 3 and the y-intercept is −5. For y = −2x + 7, the y-intercept is 7.

**Finding an equation.** Through (2, 1) and (6, 9): slope = 8 / 4 = 2, so 1 = 2(2) + b and b = −3. The equation is y = 2x − 3. A line with slope −1/2 and y-intercept 3 is y = −(1/2)x + 3.

**Linear models.** A cost with a fixed part and a variable part is C = fixed + rate × n. For C = 20 + 5n with n = 6, C = $50. A taxi charging $4 plus $1.50 per km costs 4 + 1.50 × 10 = $19 for 10 km.
