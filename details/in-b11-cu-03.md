# Mitochondria, plastids, ribosomes and the cytoskeleton

Mitochondria (cristae, matrix, ATP synthesis) and chloroplasts (grana, stroma) are double-membrane, semi-autonomous organelles with their own DNA and 70S ribosomes. Eukaryotic ribosomes are 80S. The cytoskeleton has microtubules, microfilaments and intermediate filaments; cilia and flagella have a 9 + 2 arrangement.

Plastids are chloroplasts, chromoplasts and leucoplasts.
