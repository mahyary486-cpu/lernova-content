# Subatomic particles and early atomic models

The atom has electrons (Thomson), a nucleus of protons and neutrons (Rutherford, Chadwick). The atomic number is the number of protons and the mass number is the sum of protons and neutrons. Isotopes have the same atomic number; isobars the same mass number.

The electron charge is −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
