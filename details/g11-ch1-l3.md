# Transformations of Functions

## The basic rules
Start with the graph of y = f(x). Each change below moves or reshapes the graph.
| Change | What happens to the graph |
|---|---|
| f(x) + k | shifts **up** k units (down if k is negative) |
| f(x - h) | shifts **right** h units (left if h is negative) |
| -f(x) | reflects over the **x-axis** |
| f(-x) | reflects over the **y-axis** |
| a * f(x), a > 1 | **vertical stretch** by a factor of a |
| a * f(x), 0 < a < 1 | **vertical shrink** by a factor of a |
| f(bx), b > 1 | **horizontal shrink** by a factor of 1/b |

## Watch the direction
Changes **outside** the function (like + k) act the way you expect. Changes **inside** the parentheses (like x - h) act the opposite way: f(x + 4) moves the graph **left** 4, and f(x - 2) moves it **right** 2.

## Vertex form of a parabola
y = a(x - h)^2 + k has its vertex at **(h, k)**. If a is negative, the parabola opens down. If a is bigger than 1 it is narrower than y = x^2.

## Moving a point
If (x, y) is on the graph of f, then the point on g(x) = f(x - h) + k is **(x + h, y + k)**.

## Order of transformations
Do stretches and reflections first, then shifts.

## Worked examples
1. g(x) = (x - 3)^2 is y = x^2 shifted right 3. Vertex **(3, 0)**.
2. g(x) = -2(x + 1)^2 + 5. Vertex (-1, 5). Since a = -2 the parabola opens **down** and is stretched.
3. (4, 6) is on f. On g(x) = f(x - 1) + 2 the point becomes (4 + 1, 6 + 2) = **(5, 8)**.

## Common mistakes
- Moving f(x + 4) to the right. Plus inside means **left**.
- Applying the shift before the reflection or stretch. Do stretch and reflection first.
- Mixing up the two reflections: -f(x) flips up and down, f(-x) flips left and right.
