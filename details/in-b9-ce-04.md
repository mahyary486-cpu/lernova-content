# Movement of substances across the cell membrane

Diffusion is the movement of particles from high to low concentration. Osmosis is the movement of water through a semi-permeable membrane.

In a hypotonic solution a cell swells because water enters; in a hypertonic solution it shrinks (plasmolysis); in an isotonic solution there is no net movement. Raisins swell in water and red blood cells shrink in strong salt water. Active transport moves substances against a gradient and needs energy (ATP).
