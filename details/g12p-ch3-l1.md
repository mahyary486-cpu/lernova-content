# Magnetic Fields and Forces on Moving Charges

Charged particles from the Sun are bent by the Earth's field and spiral around its field lines, producing auroras.

## Key ideas
- Unit: **tesla (T)**; field lines go from **N to S** outside a magnet.
- **F = q v B sin(theta)**, perpendicular to v and B.
- The magnetic force does **no work**; it changes direction only.
- Circular motion: **r = m v / (q B)**; **T = 2 pi m / (q B)**.
- The force is zero if **v is parallel to B**.
- Use the **right-hand rule** (reverse for negative charges).

## Worked examples
**Example 1: Force.** A proton (1.6 x 10^-19 C) moves at 2 x 10^6 m/s perpendicular to a 0.5 T field.
1. F = q v B = 1.6 x 10^-19 x 2 x 10^6 x 0.5.
2. F = 1.6 x 10^-13 N.

**Example 2: Radius.** An electron (9.1 x 10^-31 kg) moves at 1 x 10^7 m/s in a 1 x 10^-3 T field.
1. r = m v / (q B) = 9.1 x 10^-31 x 10^7 / (1.6 x 10^-19 x 10^-3).
2. r = 0.057 m = 5.7 cm.

**Example 3: Period.** Find the period of a proton (1.67 x 10^-27 kg) in a 1 T field.
1. T = 2 pi m / (q B) = 6.28 x 1.67 x 10^-27 / (1.6 x 10^-19).
2. T = 6.6 x 10^-8 s.

## Common mistakes
- Thinking the magnetic force speeds up the particle.
- Forgetting the sign of the charge.
- Using the angle between v and B wrongly.
