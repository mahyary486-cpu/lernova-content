# Passive voice no futuro, com modais e no presente contínuo

## Futuro

**will be + particípio**: *Dinner will be served at eight.* / *A new bridge will be built next year.*

## Modais

**modal + be + particípio**:

- This medicine **should be taken** twice a day.
- The homework **must be finished** today.

## Present Continuous

**am/is/are being + particípio**: *The road is being repaired.* (Active: Someone is repairing the road.)

## Present Perfect

**has/have been + particípio**: *The project has been finished.* / *The windows haven't been cleaned yet.*

## Perguntas

*Were you invited?* (Active: Did they invite you?) / *When was America discovered?*

## Outras construções

- *He is said to be rich.* (= People say that he is rich.)
- *He got fired* significa *he was fired* (uso informal de **get**).

## Atenção

O verbo to be concorda com o sujeito da passiva: *The letters were typed.*
