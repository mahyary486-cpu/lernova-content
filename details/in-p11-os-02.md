# Velocity and acceleration in SHM

v = ω√(A² − x²) with maximum ωA at the mean position and zero at the extremes; a = −ω²x with maximum ω²A at the extremes and zero at the mean position. Velocity leads displacement by π/2 and acceleration is in antiphase with displacement.

Example: A = 0.1 m and ω = 10 rad/s give v_max = 1 m/s.
