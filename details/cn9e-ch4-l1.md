# 宾语从句的引导词

宾语从句在复合句中作**宾语**，放在及物动词（或介词）之后。

## 三类引导词

1. **that**：陈述句，在口语中常**省略**；
   - I think (that) he is right.
   - She said (that) she was tired.
2. **if / whether**：一般疑问句，意为"是否"；
   - I don't know **if / whether** he will come.
   - I wonder **whether** it will rain.
3. **疑问词**（what, who, when, where, why, how）：特殊疑问句，疑问词本身保留。
   - Could you tell me **where** the post office is?
   - I don't know **what** he wants.

## 注意

- **whether** 可以与 or not 连用：I don't know whether he will come **or not**；
- 介词后只能用 **whether**，不用 if：It depends on **whether** he agrees.
- 动词 ask、wonder、know、tell、see 后常接 if / whether 或疑问词。
