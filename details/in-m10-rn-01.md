# Euclid's division lemma and algorithm

Euclid's division lemma: for positive integers a and b there are unique integers q and r with a = bq + r and 0 ≤ r < b. Repeating the division with the divisor and remainder until the remainder is 0 gives Euclid's division algorithm; the last divisor is the HCF.

Example: 225 = 135 × 1 + 90, 135 = 90 × 1 + 45, 90 = 45 × 2, so HCF(135, 225) = 45. The lemma also shows, for instance, that every odd positive integer has the form 2q + 1.
