# Solving linear equations

To solve an equation, do the same operation to both sides until the variable is alone.

**One step.** x + 7 = 12 gives x = 5.

**Two steps.** 3x − 5 = 10 gives 3x = 15, so x = 5.

**Brackets.** 2(x − 3) = 10 gives 2x − 6 = 10, so 2x = 16 and x = 8.

**Fractions.** x/3 + 2 = 5 gives x/3 = 3, so x = 9.

**Check** by substituting your answer back into the original equation.
