# Areas of similar triangles and Pythagoras theorem

The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles equals the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides. If the sides are in ratio 3 : 5, the areas are in ratio 9 : 25.

In a right triangle, hypotenuse² = sum of the squares of the other two sides (c² = a² + b²); the converse also holds. For example 5, 12, 13 is a right triangle.
