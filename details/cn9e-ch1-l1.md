# 一般现在时与现在进行时

## 一般现在时

表示**经常性、习惯性的动作**、客观事实和真理。

- 标志词：always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day
- 主语是第三人称单数时，动词加 **-s / -es**：goes, watches, studies, has

**例句：**

- She **goes** to school by bike every day.
- The sun **rises** in the east.

## 现在进行时

表示**现在正在进行**的动作。结构：**am / is / are + 动词 -ing**。

- 标志词：now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!

**例句：**

- Look! The children **are playing** in the park.
- I **am doing** my homework now.

## 对比

| 时态 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 | 习惯、经常 | He plays football every day. |
| 现在进行时 | 此刻正在 | He is playing football now. |
